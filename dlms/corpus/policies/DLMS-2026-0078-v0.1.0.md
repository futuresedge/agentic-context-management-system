## SUMMARY
This policy defines the detection, classification, response, and
escalation requirements when a metric crosses a warning or breach
threshold as defined in any stage SLA policy (DLMS-2026-0057 through
DLMS-2026-0062). It establishes mandatory response windows, assigns
responsibility at each severity tier, and governs how persistent breach
patterns are surfaced through the learning synthesis process.

## SCOPE

Applies to:
- All metrics governed by stage SLA policies (DLMS-2026-0057 through
  DLMS-2026-0061) and the Context Delivery SLA (DLMS-2026-0062)
- All stage metrics collector agents, stage orchestrator agents,
  agent:sla-manager, agent:anomaly-detector, and
  agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator
- sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin in their escalation acknowledgement role
- Any anomaly report (AR-) that captures a metric breach as a detected
  anomaly

Excludes:
- Breaches of CI cycle timing, which are governed by CI Cycle Policy
  (DLMS-2026-0076)
- Template or document authoring quality failures, which are governed
  by Quality Threshold Policy (DLMS-2026-0043)
- Breaches identified retrospectively during a CI cycle but not
  flagged in real-time (those are handled solely through the anomaly
  report in the relevant CI cycle's AR-)

## DEFINITIONS

breach event:
  An audit event emitted by a stage metrics collector when a metric
  crosses its breach threshold as defined in the relevant SLA policy.
  A breach event is distinct from a warning event; each has its own
  response requirements.

warning event:
  An audit event emitted when a metric crosses its warning threshold
  but not yet its breach threshold.

breach threshold:
  The metric value beyond which the relevant SLA policy defines the
  condition as a breach. Thresholds are defined in DLMS-2026-0057
  through DLMS-2026-0062.

warning threshold:
  The metric value beyond which the relevant SLA policy defines a
  warning condition. Warning thresholds are below breach thresholds and
  serve as early indicators.

resolution window:
  The maximum elapsed time from breach event emission to documented
  resolution or escalation. Resolution windows are tier-dependent:
  warning tier — 24 hours; breach tier — 8 hours; critical tier — 2
  hours.

persistent breach:
  A breach of the same metric in the same stage in three or more
  consecutive CI cycles, regardless of whether each individual breach
  was resolved within its resolution window.

## RULES

### Detection and Emission

R01:
  rule: >
    A stage metrics collector must emit a breach event to the audit
    trail (per Audit Trail Policy DLMS-2026-0015) within 15 minutes of
    a metric crossing its breach threshold as defined in the relevant
    stage SLA policy (DLMS-2026-0057 through DLMS-2026-0062). A warning
    event must be emitted within 15 minutes of a metric crossing its
    warning threshold.
  test: >
    breach_event.emitted_at - metric.threshold_crossed_at <= 15 minutes
    FOR each metric in [DLMS-2026-0057 through DLMS-2026-0062]
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; agent:anomaly-detector

R02:
  rule: >
    Every breach event must include: (a) the metric identifier and SLA
    policy doc_id; (b) the observed value; (c) the breach threshold
    value; (d) the timestamp of threshold crossing; (e) the stage or
    domain affected; and (f) the emitting agent identity. Incomplete
    breach events are treated as missing and trigger the same response
    as a failure to emit.
  test: >
    breach_event.metric_id non-null AND
    breach_event.sla_policy_ref IN [DLMS-2026-0057...DLMS-2026-0062] AND
    breach_event.observed_value non-null AND
    breach_event.threshold_value non-null AND
    breach_event.threshold_crossed_at non-null AND
    breach_event.stage non-null AND
    breach_event.emitted_by non-null
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Format Validator

### Warning Tier Response

R03:
  rule: >
    On receipt of a warning event, the relevant stage orchestrator agent
    must be notified within 30 minutes. The stage orchestrator must log
    an acknowledgement and begin monitoring for breach threshold crossing.
    If the metric returns to within-target range within 24 hours, the
    stage orchestrator logs resolution. If the metric crosses the breach
    threshold within 24 hours, the breach tier response (R04–R06) triggers
    immediately regardless of remaining warning window.
  test: >
    stage_orchestrator.notified_at - warning_event.emitted_at <= 30 min AND
    acknowledgement logged within 30 min of notification AND
    IF metric.value crosses breach_threshold WITHIN 24h:
      breach tier response triggered immediately
  checked_by: stage_orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Breach Tier Response

R04:
  rule: >
    On receipt of a breach event, agent:sla-manager must acknowledge
    the breach within 8 hours of event emission. Acknowledgement must
    be recorded as an audit event citing the breach event reference.
    Failure to acknowledge within 8 hours triggers the critical tier
    response (R05–R06).
  test: >
    acknowledgement.recorded_at - breach_event.emitted_at <= 8h AND
    acknowledgement.breach_event_ref == breach_event.id AND
    IF acknowledgement.recorded_at - breach_event.emitted_at > 8h:
      critical tier response triggered
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

R05:
  rule: >
    On acknowledging a breach event, agent:sla-manager must initiate
    a corrective review. The corrective review must: (a) identify the
    root cause of the breach; (b) determine whether the breach can be
    resolved within the current CI cycle; (c) produce a finding recorded
    in the audit trail. If resolved, agent:sla-manager logs resolution
    to close the breach event. If not resolved within 8 hours of
    acknowledgement, the critical tier response triggers.
  test: >
    corrective_review.initiated_at non-null AND
    corrective_review.root_cause recorded AND
    corrective_review.resolution_feasible: boolean recorded AND
    IF corrective_review.resolved_at - acknowledgement.recorded_at > 8h
       OR corrective_review.resolution_feasible == false:
      critical tier response triggered
  checked_by: agent:sla-manager; Audit Trail Agent

### Critical Tier Response

R06:
  rule: >
    Critical tier response is triggered when: (a) a breach persists for
    more than 2 hours after acknowledgement without documented progress
    toward resolution; or (b) any anomaly report (AR-) records an anomaly
    with severity: critical linked to the breaching metric; or (c) the
    8-hour acknowledgement window (R04) is missed. On critical tier
    trigger, agent:sla-manager must notify sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin within
    30 minutes. sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin has a 2-hour response window per
    SysAdmin Escalation Policy (DLMS-2026-0080, Wave 13).
  test: >
    IF (now - acknowledgement.recorded_at > 2h AND no resolution) OR
       AR-.anomaly.severity == "critical" FOR breaching metric OR
       acknowledgement missed:
      notification_to_sysadmin.dispatched_at - trigger.at <= 30 min
  checked_by: agent:sla-manager; agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

### Anomaly Report Linkage

R07:
  rule: >
    Any metric at or above its breach threshold at the time an anomaly
    report (AR-) is produced for the relevant CI cycle must be captured
    as an anomaly entry in that AR- with severity: warning or
    severity: critical as applicable. agent:anomaly-detector must not
    produce an AR- that omits a breach-threshold metric that was active
    during the report's period.
  test: >
    FOR each metric in breach OR warning at cycle period:
      EXISTS anomaly entry in AR- where anomaly.metric == metric.id AND
      anomaly.severity <= matching threshold level
  checked_by: Agent Format Validator; agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

### Audit Trail

R08:
  rule: >
    All breach events, warning events, acknowledgements, corrective
    review findings, escalations, and resolutions must be logged to the
    audit trail in accordance with Audit Trail Policy (DLMS-2026-0015).
    No breach response action may be considered complete without a
    corresponding audit entry.
  test: >
    FOR each response action in [warning event, breach event, acknowledgement,
      corrective_review, escalation, resolution]:
      audit_entry.action_ref == response_action.id AND
      audit_entry.timestamp non-null AND
      audit_entry.actor non-null
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

### Persistent Breach Pattern

R09:
  rule: >
    A breach of the same metric in the same stage across three or more
    consecutive CI cycles constitutes a persistent breach. Agent:sla-manager
    must flag the persistent breach to agent:learning-synthesizer via the
    knowledge base contribution process (DLMS-2026-0063) so the pattern
    is available for learning synthesis (DLMS-2026-0064). Persistent
    breach patterns must appear in the next learning synthesis artefact
    as an unactioned_critical_major entry if flagged at severity: critical
    or major, or as a frequency_spike entry if at severity: warning across
    cycles.
  test: >
    IF same metric in same stage breached in cycles N, N+1, N+2:
      kb_contribution submitted to agent:learning-synthesizer AND
      knowledge_base entry created per DLMS-2026-0063
  checked_by: agent:sla-manager; agent:learning-synthesizer

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS

Exceptions require Tier 3 exception approval from sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin:
1. Exception request must be submitted as an audit event before the
   non-compliant response window expires, with the specific rule,
   the reason, and a proposed compensating control.
2. sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin must acknowledge within 2 hours for
   critical-tier exceptions, 8 hours for breach-tier, 24 hours for
   warning-tier.
3. Exceptions apply to a single breach event instance only and do not
   create standing waivers.

The following cannot be excepted:
- R08 (audit trail logging — Tier 2 per DLMS-2026-0015)
- R02 element (e): emitting agent identity must always be recorded

## ENFORCEMENT

primary_agent:    agent:sla-manager
secondary_agent:  agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; stage metrics collector agents
gate_positions:
  - 15-minute emission deadline after threshold crossing (R01)
  - 8-hour acknowledgement deadline after breach event (R04)
  - 30-minute critical-tier notification deadline after trigger (R06)
violation_type:   Missed response window — audit event recorded;
                  sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin notified; breach escalated to
                  next response tier automatically.
escalation_ref:   SysAdmin Escalation Policy (DLMS-2026-0080, Wave 13)

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE

interval_max:     6 months
trigger_conditions:
  - Any SLA policy (DLMS-2026-0057 through DLMS-2026-0062) is updated
    with new or revised metric thresholds
  - A persistent breach (R09) is identified in three consecutive CI
    cycles that this policy did not prevent
  - Any response window defined in this policy is consistently missed
    across two or more consecutive CI cycles
review_owner:     sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
review_process:   Standard policy review per DLMS-2026-0001
