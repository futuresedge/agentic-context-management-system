## SUMMARY
This policy governs the minimum evidence standards, reviewer qualification
criteria, verification record requirements, and escalation timeframes for
all DLMS document review sessions. It operates alongside Multi-Perspective
Review Policy (DLMS-2026-0017), which governs lens selection and verdict
rules; this policy governs what constitutes a valid review record and the
conditions under which a stalled or blocked review must escalate. This is
a Tier 3 Agent Behaviour policy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all Perspective Reviewer agents; the Review Stage Orchestrator;
the Review Synthesizer; the Approval Gate Agent; the Audit Trail Agent; all
documents undergoing the DLMS review stage.

Excludes: the rules governing which lenses are mandatory, lens verdict logic,
and conflict resolution procedures — all defined in Multi-Perspective Review
Policy (DLMS-2026-0017). Excludes internal agent deliberation that does not
produce a verification record. Excludes structural conformance assessment of
verification records, which is governed by Quality Threshold Policy
(DLMS-2026-0043). Excludes throughput performance targets, which are deferred
to Stage SLA Policy (Wave 10).

## DEFINITIONS

blocked review:
  A review session in which at least one Perspective Reviewer has returned a
  fail verdict on a mandatory lens (per DLMS-2026-0017 R06–R07), or in which
  an unresolved conflict between lens verdicts has been surfaced by the Review
  Synthesizer (per DLMS-2026-0017 R09). A blocked review cannot close without
  either a return-to-author directive or a SysAdmin resolution directive.

evidence citation:
  A direct reference to a specific field value, quoted passage (maximum
  60 words), section identifier, or structural fact within the document under
  review. An evidence citation must be sufficient for an independent reader to
  locate the cited element without additional context. Paraphrase, summary,
  and assessments of likely author intent are not evidence citations.

Perspective Reviewer:
  An agent instantiated to evaluate a document from a single assigned lens,
  producing a verification record as its sole output. Each Perspective Reviewer
  is a distinct agent invocation; simultaneous instantiations of the same
  agent class reviewing the same document are each independently qualifying
  as separate Perspective Reviewers.

review session:
  The bounded work unit beginning when the Review Stage Orchestrator dispatches
  Perspective Reviewers for a document and ending when the Review Synthesizer
  produces a synthesis artefact or the session is escalated to SysAdmin. Each
  document version under review constitutes one review session.

stalled review:
  A review session in which one or more Perspective Reviewers have not
  returned a verification record within the period defined in R15. A stalled
  review is a precursor condition to a blocked review and triggers active
  investigation by the Review Stage Orchestrator.

synthesis artefact:
  The structured output produced by the Review Synthesizer consolidating all
  lens verdicts, evidence citations, conflicts, and a final recommendation
  (advance | block). The synthesis artefact is the sole authoritative input
  to the Approval Gate Agent.

verification record:
  A document produced by a Perspective Reviewer conforming to
  TMPL-verification-001 (DLMS-2026-0030). Each Perspective Reviewer produces
  exactly one verification record per review session. Verification records
  are permanent, immutable artefacts.

## RULES

### Pre-Review_Prerequisites

R01:
  rule: >
    The Review Stage Orchestrator must confirm that the document carries
    status: under_review before dispatching any Perspective Reviewer. A
    document in any status other than under_review must not enter the
    review stage. Status: under_review may only be set per Document Status
    Transition Policy (DLMS-2026-0037).
  test: >
    At review-session-opened event: document.status == 'under_review'.
    If document.status ≠ 'under_review', a review-blocked audit event is
    produced citing the status violation and no Perspective Reviewer is
    dispatched.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R02:
  rule: >
    The Review Stage Orchestrator must confirm that a verified creation DoD
    record exists (dod-passed event for stage: creation) for the document
    before dispatching any Perspective Reviewer. A document without a
    completed creation gate must not enter review.
  test: >
    At review-session-opened event: a dod-passed audit event for
    stage: creation exists for this doc_id with timestamp preceding the
    review-session-opened event.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Evidence_Standards

R03:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer must include at least one evidence citation per
    evaluated criterion in its verification record. Evidence citations must
    be direct references to specific content within the document under
    review: a field value, a quoted passage (maximum 60 words), a section
    identifier, or a structural fact (e.g., count of rules, presence or
    absence of a section).
  test: >
    For each verification record: count(evidence_citations) ≥ 1 per
    evaluated criterion. A verification record with zero citations for any
    evaluated criterion fails the evidence standard and is treated as
    non-conformant per Quality Threshold Policy (DLMS-2026-0043).
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Quality Monitor

R04:
  rule: >
    Opinions and assessments unsupported by evidence citations are
    non-conformant. A Perspective Reviewer must not submit a verification
    record in which any verdict is supported only by inference, paraphrase,
    or an assessment of the author's likely intent. The Review Synthesizer
    rejects such records as incomplete.
  test: >
    Review Synthesizer validates: each verdict in the verification record
    has at least one evidence_citation whose target is locatable in the
    current version of the document. Non-locatable citations are flagged
    as unsupported and the record treated as non-conformant.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer

R05:
  rule: >
    A Perspective Reviewer must not infer author intent beyond what the
    document states explicitly. The reviewer evaluates the document as it
    exists, not as it was intended to be read. Where a document's meaning
    is ambiguous, the reviewer must render a fail or pass-with-observations
    verdict citing the specific ambiguity, not a pass based on charitable
    interpretation.
  test: >
    Verification records must not reference information sourced from: author
    communications, prior version content not present in the current version,
    or knowledge of the document's intended purpose not explicitly stated in
    the document. Review Synthesizer flags any such references.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Audit Trail Agent

### Reviewer_Qualification

R06:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer must hold a read tool for the document class
    under review but must not hold a submit tool, write tool, or author tool
    for the same document. A reviewer with execution capability over the
    reviewed artefact must not be dispatched. The Review Stage Orchestrator
    must confirm tool qualification before dispatch.
  test: >
    For each dispatched Perspective Reviewer: tool_set contains
    read:[doc-class] AND does NOT contain submit:[doc], write:[doc], or
    author:[doc].
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R07:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer's agent identity must be distinct from the
    document's created_by value and from any agent that contributed to
    the document's content in the current version. This is the
    operationalisation of Verification Independence Policy
    (DLMS-2026-0014 R05–R06) at the review stage.
  test: >
    For each Perspective Reviewer: reviewer_agent_id ≠ document.created_by
    AND reviewer_agent_id is not present in the audit trail as a write or
    author event for this doc_id at the current version.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Approval Gate Agent

### Verification_Record_Standard

R08:
  rule: >
    Every Perspective Reviewer must produce exactly one verification record
    per review session, conforming to TMPL-verification-001
    (DLMS-2026-0030). A reviewer that completes its evaluation without
    producing a conformant verification record has not completed its review.
    The review session remains open for that lens until a conformant record
    is received.
  test: >
    For each dispatched Perspective Reviewer: exactly one verification
    record exists for the review session with template_ref: DLMS-2026-0030
    and all mandatory fields of DLMS-2026-0030 populated.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Review Synthesizer

R09:
  rule: >
    Each verification record must carry at minimum: reviewer_agent_id,
    assigned_lens, verdict (pass | pass-with-observations | fail),
    evidence_citations[], and timestamp. A record missing any of these
    five elements is non-conformant and is treated by the Review
    Synthesizer as a fail for the assigned lens.
  test: >
    For each verification record: reviewer_agent_id is non-null AND
    assigned_lens is non-null AND verdict is one of [pass,
    pass-with-observations, fail] AND evidence_citations is a non-empty
    array AND timestamp is valid ISO 8601.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Quality Monitor

R10:
  rule: >
    A verification record with verdict: fail must include a non-null
    fail_reason field that cites the specific criterion or document element
    that failed evaluation. A fail verdict without a cited fail_reason
    is non-conformant and is escalated to the SysAdmin queue as a
    malformed record.
  test: >
    For all verification records where verdict == fail: fail_reason is
    non-null AND contains at least one evidence citation. Null fail_reason
    on a fail verdict triggers a review-malformed-record audit event and
    SysAdmin queue entry.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Audit Trail Agent

### Quorum_and_Synthesis

R11:
  rule: >
    All five mandatory lenses defined in DLMS-2026-0017 R01 must produce
    valid, conformant verification records before the Review Synthesizer
    may begin synthesis. A missing or non-conformant record is treated as
    a fail for that lens. Synthesis does not proceed with fewer than five
    valid mandatory lens records.
  test: >
    Review Synthesizer activation precondition:
    count(conformant_verification_records for mandatory lenses) == 5
    AND each record's assigned_lens matches one of the five mandatory
    lens identifiers from DLMS-2026-0017.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Review Stage Orchestrator

R12:
  rule: >
    The Review Synthesizer is the sole agent authorised to produce the
    synthesis artefact. The synthesis artefact must record: all five
    mandatory lens verdicts, all pass-with-observations observations,
    any identified conflicts between lens assessments, conflict
    resolution outcomes or escalation status, and a final recommendation
    (advance | block) with rationale.
  test: >
    authored_by field of synthesis artefact == review-synthesizer:[instance_id].
    Synthesis artefact contains all five mandatory lens verdicts AND
    final_recommendation is one of [advance, block].
  checked_by: Approval Gate Agent; Audit Trail Agent

R13:
  rule: >
    The synthesis artefact is the sole input to the Approval Gate Agent's
    decision. The Approval Gate Agent must not re-examine individual
    verification records or read the document content directly. Its
    decision is based exclusively on the synthesis artefact's final
    recommendation.
  test: >
    Approval Gate Agent audit trail during evaluation: all reads reference
    the synthesis artefact only. Reads of individual verification records
    or the document body by the Approval Gate Agent constitute a context
    boundary violation per DLMS-2026-0016.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Escalation_Timeframes

R14:
  rule: >
    A review session with status: blocked must receive either a
    return-to-author directive or a SysAdmin resolution directive within
    48 hours of the review-session-blocked audit event. If no directive
    is issued within 48 hours of the blocked event, the Review Stage
    Orchestrator must produce a review-escalation-timeout event and
    escalate automatically to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin. This threshold is
    provisional pending Stage SLA Policy (Wave 10); see REVIEW_SCHEDULE.
  test: >
    For each review session with status: blocked:
    timestamp(directive) ≤ blocked_at + 48h.
    IF no directive within 48h: review-escalation-timeout event logged
    with doc_id, session_id, blocked_at, elapsed_hours AND a SysAdmin
    escalation ticket is produced.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R15:
  rule: >
    A review session in which not all five mandatory lens records have
    been received within 72 hours of the review-session-opened event
    must produce a review-stalled audit event. The Review Stage
    Orchestrator must investigate the outstanding reviewer(s) and either
    re-dispatch a qualified substitute reviewer or escalate to SysAdmin.
    This threshold is provisional pending Stage SLA Policy (Wave 10);
    see REVIEW_SCHEDULE.
  test: >
    For each review session:
    IF (now - review-session-opened.timestamp) > 72h AND
    count(received_valid_lens_records) < 5 THEN
    review-stalled audit event exists with doc_id, session_id,
    open_lenses[], and elapsed_hours.
  checked_by: Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Context_Boundary

R16:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer agent must operate within a strict context
    boundary per Agent Context Boundary Policy (DLMS-2026-0016). Its
    reads list must contain only: the document under review AND the
    template that governs the document's doc_type AND the lens description
    for its single assigned lens. A Perspective Reviewer must not read
    other reviewers' in-progress or completed verification records, the
    document's prior version files, or the author's creation context artefacts.
  test: >
    All reads by a Perspective Reviewer during a review session are
    confined to: target doc_id current version AND applicable doc_type
    template AND assigned lens description. Any read outside this set
    is a context boundary violation per DLMS-2026-0016.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Audit_Requirements

R17:
  rule: >
    The following audit events are required for all review sessions:
    review-session-opened at session start (includes doc_id, session_id,
    timestamp, dispatched_lenses[]); review-session-completed on synthesis
    artefact production (includes final_recommendation); review-session-blocked
    on any blocking fail or unresolved conflict (includes blocking_reason).
  test: >
    For each review session: review-session-opened event exists at session
    start. On synthesis production: review-session-completed event exists.
    On any block: review-session-blocked event exists with non-null
    blocking_reason. All events include doc_id, session_id, and timestamp.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Tier 3 exceptions are permitted on a time-limited basis with SysAdmin
approval. To request an exception to any rule in this policy: (1) The
requesting agent produces a policy-exception-request artefact addressed
to the SysAdmin directive queue, citing the rule reference, the reason
the rule cannot be satisfied, and the proposed mitigating controls.
(2) sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin reviews and, if approved, issues a
policy-exception-grant directive specifying the rule, scope, expiry date,
and audit conditions. (3) The exception is logged as a policy-exception
audit event and is visible in the next CI cycle report. No exception
may extend beyond 90 days without renewal. No exception may waive R03,
R07, or R11. The escalation timeframe thresholds in R14 and R15 may be
temporarily adjusted by SysAdmin directive pending Wave 10 formalisation.

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Review Stage Orchestrator; Review Synthesizer;
                    Approval Gate Agent; Audit Trail Agent
  gate_positions:
    - Pre-review gate: Review Stage Orchestrator validates R01–R02
      before dispatching reviewers
    - Per-reviewer: Review Synthesizer validates R03–R10 on receipt of
      each verification record
    - Synthesis gate: Review Synthesizer validates R11–R12 before
      producing synthesis artefact
    - Approval gate: Approval Gate Agent enforces R13 (synthesis-only
      input) and verifies independence per DLMS-2026-0014
    - Escalation monitoring: Review Stage Orchestrator monitors R14–R15
      timeframes continuously during active review sessions
  audit: >
    All review session lifecycle events are logged per R17. Evidence
    citation validation outcomes are logged per review session.
    Escalation timeout events are logged as permanent audit trail
    entries. Context boundary compliance for all reviewer agents is
    logged per DLMS-2026-0015.
  violation_type:   Tier 3 operational violation
  escalation: >
    Reviewer qualification violations (R06–R07) are immediately escalated
    to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin. Approval Gate Agent context boundary
    violations (R13) are immediately escalated. Escalation timeframe
    breaches (R14–R15) produce automatic SysAdmin alerts. All other
    violations are flagged in the next CI cycle report.

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      6 months (Tier 3 maximum)
  trigger_conditions:
    - SCHEDULED OBLIGATION: Stage SLA Policy (Wave 10) is drafted —
      the provisional escalation thresholds in R14 (48h) and R15 (72h)
      must be reconciled with the formalised SLA targets and this policy
      updated via PATCH version increment. This review is mandatory.
    - Multi-Perspective Review Policy (DLMS-2026-0017) is amended in a
      way that affects the mandatory lens set, verdict rules, or conflict
      resolution procedures
    - TMPL-verification-001 (DLMS-2026-0030) is amended in a way that
      affects mandatory fields or verdict vocabulary
    - Systematic evidence citation non-conformance pattern is detected
      by CI suggesting the citation standard requires clarification
    - A new lens type is introduced that changes the quorum threshold
