## SUMMARY
This policy defines the minimum structural quality thresholds for verification
records and Definition of Done records produced in the DLMS pipeline, specifies
unconditional hard-fail criteria, and establishes measurement obligations for
the Quality Monitor agent. Time thresholds previously carried as provisional
are now formalised in Stage SLA documents DLMS-2026-0057 through 0061. This
is a Tier 3 Agent Behaviour policy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all verification records produced by Perspective Reviewer agents;
all DoD records produced at each of the six stage gates; the Quality Monitor
agent; CI cycle reports; all DLMS documents regardless of doc_type.

Excludes: quality assessment of document body content or policy substance —
whether a policy's rules are well-constructed, adequately scoped, or
correctly implemented is a matter for Perspective Reviewers applying defined
lenses, not this policy. This policy governs only the structural conformance
of verification and DoD artefacts. Excludes pipeline throughput and turnaround
time performance, which are deferred to Stage SLA Policy (Wave 10).

## DEFINITIONS

conformant:
  A verification record or DoD record that satisfies all mandatory field
  requirements of its governing template — TMPL-verification-001
  (DLMS-2026-0030) for verification records, or the applicable stage DoD
  template (DLMS-2026-0024 through DLMS-2026-0029) for DoD records — with
  no required field null or absent and all constrained fields carrying valid
  values. Conformance is binary: a record is conformant or it is not.

hard-fail criterion:
  A conformance criterion whose failure immediately produces a
  quality-threshold-fail outcome and blocks stage advancement. Hard-fail
  criteria are enumerated in R03–R08. They cannot be waived by exception,
  override, or SysAdmin directive.

non-conformant:
  A verification record or DoD record that fails one or more required field
  constraints of its governing template. A non-conformant record is treated
  as a fail verdict regardless of the quality of the underlying review work.
  The producing agent is responsible for supplying a corrected, conformant
  replacement.

quality-threshold-fail:
  An audit event type indicating that a verification record or DoD record
  has failed one or more hard-fail criteria. This event triggers remediation
  by the producing agent. The underlying work product need not be re-executed;
  only the non-conformant artefact is replaced.

## RULES

### Threshold_Scope

R01:
  rule: >
    The quality thresholds in this policy apply exclusively to the structural
    conformance of verification records (artefacts produced per
    DLMS-2026-0030) and DoD records (artefacts produced per DLMS-2026-0024
    through DLMS-2026-0029). This policy does not govern subjective quality
    assessments of document content, policy adequacy, or rule construction.
  test: >
    Quality Monitor applies thresholds only to: verification records with
    template_ref: DLMS-2026-0030 AND DoD records with template_ref matching
    one of DLMS-2026-0024 through DLMS-2026-0029. Threshold checks against
    document body content are prohibited.
  checked_by: Quality Monitor; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Verification_Record_Conformance

R02:
  rule: >
    A verification record is conformant only when all mandatory fields
    defined in TMPL-verification-001 (DLMS-2026-0030) are populated with
    non-null values. A record with any mandatory field null or absent is
    non-conformant and is treated as a fail verdict for the assigned lens,
    regardless of the reviewer's intended verdict.
  test: >
    For each verification record: count of non-null mandatory fields ==
    count of mandatory fields in DLMS-2026-0030 SCHEMA. Any field count
    mismatch or null mandatory field triggers quality-threshold-fail.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Quality Monitor

R03:
  rule: >
    A verification record's verdict field must carry a valid enum value as
    defined in DLMS-2026-0030. A null verdict, an absent verdict, or a
    free-text verdict that does not match the controlled vocabulary is a
    hard-fail criterion and produces a quality-threshold-fail event.
  test: >
    verification_record.verdict in [pass, pass-with-observations, fail].
    Any other value triggers quality-threshold-fail event.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Quality Monitor

R04:
  rule: >
    A verification record's evidence_citations array must contain at least
    one citation per evaluated criterion. An empty evidence_citations array
    is a hard-fail criterion. A partially populated citations array — where
    any evaluated criterion has zero citations — is also a hard-fail.
  test: >
    For each criterion in verification_record.criteria_evaluated:
    count(evidence_citations targeting that criterion) ≥ 1. Any criterion
    with zero citations triggers quality-threshold-fail.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Quality Monitor

### DoD_Record_Conformance

R05:
  rule: >
    A DoD record is conformant only when all mandatory fields and all
    mandatory checklist items of its governing DoD template are present
    and carry valid values. A DoD record with any mandatory field null,
    absent, or carrying an invalid value is non-conformant and is treated
    as a fail, regardless of the number of checklist items that pass.
  test: >
    For each DoD record: all mandatory fields of the governing template
    are non-null AND all mandatory checklist items are present with
    verdict values from the template's controlled vocabulary.
  checked_by: DoD Verifier; Stage Orchestrator; Quality Monitor

### Hard_Fail_Criteria

R06:
  rule: >
    A fail verdict on any mandatory lens in a review session constitutes
    a quality failure for that review session. This is an unconditional
    hard-fail criterion. No quorum mechanism, weighting system, aggregate
    pass rate, or SysAdmin directive may override a fail on a mandatory lens.
  test: >
    For any review session: IF any verification_record where
    lens == mandatory AND verdict == fail EXISTS THEN review-session
    outcome == blocked AND no stage-advancement event is produced for
    this document version until the blocking condition is resolved.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Stage Orchestrator

R07:
  rule: >
    A fail verdict on any mandatory checklist item in a DoD record
    constitutes a quality failure for that DoD record. This is an
    unconditional hard-fail criterion regardless of how many other
    mandatory items pass. Definition of Done Policy (DLMS-2026-0041
    R11) governs the resulting stage block.
  test: >
    For any DoD record: IF any mandatory_checklist_item.verdict == fail
    THEN DoD record overall_verdict == fail AND dod-failed event produced.
  checked_by: DoD Verifier; Quality Monitor

R08:
  rule: >
    A non-conformant verification record or DoD record is treated as a
    fail verdict — not as an absent, partial, or incomplete record. An
    agent that submits a non-conformant artefact has completed the task
    with a failing outcome. The stage does not advance; the producing
    agent must supply a corrected, conformant artefact.
  test: >
    For any non-conformant artefact: quality-threshold-fail event
    references the producing agent_id AND the stage does not advance
    until a conformant replacement artefact is submitted and verified.
  checked_by: Quality Monitor; Stage Orchestrator

### Formalised_Time_Thresholds

R09:
  rule: >
    Time thresholds for DLMS stage operations are governed by the Stage
    SLA documents produced in Wave 10. All thresholds are binding and
    breaches must be logged as sla-breach audit events per the relevant
    SLA document.
    — Review stage per-lens review: 48h target (M01 of DLMS-2026-0058)
    — Review DoD verification: 24h target (M04 of DLMS-2026-0058)
    — Creation stage end-to-end cycle: 72h target (M03 of DLMS-2026-0057)
    — Creation DoD verification: 24h target (M04 of DLMS-2026-0057)
    Breach definitions, measurement methods, and breach response processes
    for these thresholds are fully specified in DLMS-2026-0057 through
    DLMS-2026-0061. This rule is retained for cross-reference only.
  test: >
    Elapsed-time measurement and breach classification are performed per
    the relevant Stage SLA (DLMS-2026-0057 for creation stage,
    DLMS-2026-0058 for review stage). Direct application of threshold
    values from this rule without reference to the governing SLA is
    deprecated as of v0.1.1.
  checked_by: Quality Monitor; Review Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent;
              Creation Stage Metrics Collector; Review Stage Metrics Collector

  test: >

    REVIEW_SCHEDULE.
  checked_by: SysAdmin; Audit Trail Agent

### Measurement_Obligation

R11:
  rule: >
    The Quality Monitor agent must track and record the following metrics
    per CI cycle at minimum: (1) verification record conformance rate
    (conformant / total produced, by lens and doc_type), (2) DoD record
    conformance rate (conformant / total produced, by stage), (3) hard-fail
    criterion breach count by criterion identifier, (4) time-threshold-exceeded
    count by threshold type and stage.
  test: >
    Each CI cycle report contains a quality_metrics block with all four
    metric categories. Absence of the quality_metrics block in a CI cycle
    report is itself a measurement failure and triggers a SysAdmin alert.
  checked_by: Quality Monitor; CI Cycle Orchestrator

### Context_Boundary

R12:
  rule: >
    The Quality Monitor agent must operate within a strict context boundary
    per Agent Context Boundary Policy (DLMS-2026-0016). Its reads list must
    contain only: the verification records and DoD records for the current
    document batch under assessment. The Quality Monitor must not read
    document body content, prior batch records, or agent deliberation
    artefacts.
  test: >
    All reads by Quality Monitor during a quality assessment task are
    confined to: current_batch verification_records AND DoD records.
    Any read outside this set is a context boundary violation per
    DLMS-2026-0016.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Audit_Requirements

R13:
  rule: >
    A quality-threshold-fail audit event must be logged whenever a hard-fail
    criterion defined in R03–R08 is triggered. The event must include:
    doc_id, artefact_type (verification-record | dod-record), criterion_ref
    (the rule identifier violated), producing_agent_id, and timestamp.
  test: >
    For each triggered hard-fail criterion: a quality-threshold-fail
    audit event exists with all five required fields populated before
    any further processing of the affected artefact.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Quality Monitor

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Tier 3 exceptions are permitted on a time-limited basis with SysAdmin
approval. To request an exception to any rule in this policy: (1) The
requesting agent produces a policy-exception-request artefact addressed
to the SysAdmin directive queue, citing the rule reference, the reason
the rule cannot be satisfied, and the proposed mitigating controls.
(2) sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin reviews and, if approved, issues a
policy-exception-grant directive specifying the rule, scope, expiry date,
and audit conditions. (3) The exception is logged as a policy-exception
audit event and is visible in the next CI cycle report. No exception may
extend beyond 90 days without renewal. No exception may waive the
hard-fail criteria in R03–R08. The provisional thresholds in R09 may be
adjusted by SysAdmin directive pending Wave 10 formalisation.

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Quality Monitor; Review Synthesizer; DoD Verifier;
                    Audit Trail Agent
  gate_positions:
    - Per-verification-record: Review Synthesizer validates R02–R04 on
      receipt of each record
    - Per-DoD-record: DoD Verifier validates R05 before rendering verdict
    - Per-review-session: Quality Monitor evaluates hard-fail criteria
      R06–R08 for each session and DoD event
    - CI cycle: Quality Monitor compiles measurement metrics (R11) for
      each CI cycle report
  audit: >
    All quality-threshold-fail events are logged with the five required
    fields per R13. Time-threshold-exceeded events are logged per R09.
    Context boundary compliance for Quality Monitor is logged per
    DLMS-2026-0015. Measurement metrics are included in all CI cycle
    reports per R11.
  violation_type:   Tier 3 operational violation
  escalation: >
    Context boundary violations by Quality Monitor (R12) are immediately
    escalated. Systematic hard-fail patterns — same criterion failing
    across multiple documents in a single CI cycle — trigger a SysAdmin
    review request. Individual quality-threshold-fail events are included
    in the CI cycle report for SysAdmin review.
