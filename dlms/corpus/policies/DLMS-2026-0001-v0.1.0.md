## SUMMARY
This policy is the authoritative governing document for all policies within the DLMS
corpus. It defines how policies are created, structured, approved, maintained, and
retired. It is self-governing and sits at the apex of the policy hierarchy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all policies in the DLMS corpus, including this document.
Applies to: all agents that create, review, approve, or consume policies.
Excludes: templates, guides, patterns, standards, and SLAs (governed by their
respective policies once created).
Bootstrap exception: DLMS-2026-0001 (this document), DLMS-2026-0002,
DLMS-2026-0003, and DLMS-2026-0004 are exempt from TMPL-policy-001 compliance
as they precede it. All subsequent policies are not exempt.

## DEFINITIONS
policy:
  A document that states rules. Rules are enumerated, testable, and binary
  (compliant or non-compliant). A policy does not explain how to comply —
  that is a procedure or guide.

principles-derived policy:
  A policy whose rules directly enforce one or more of the 11 system design
  principles. These are non-overridable.

bootstrap policy:
  A Wave-1 founding document created before the DLMS pipeline existed. There
  are exactly four. No further bootstrap policies may be created.

operational policy:
  Any policy that is not meta-governance (Tier 1) or principles-derived (Tier 2).

exception:
  A time-limited, approved deviation from a specific policy rule for a specific
  document, agent, or scenario.

retirement:
  The act of moving a policy to archived status. Retired policies are never
  deleted.

## RULES

R01:
  rule: >
    All policies except bootstrap policies must be authored using TMPL-policy-001
    at the version current at the time of creation.
  test: template_id == TMPL-policy-001 OR doc_id IN [bootstrap list]
  compliance_check: Template Validator agent

R02:
  rule: >
    No policy may enter the Review stage until a Definition of Done has been
    created and approved for that document.
  test: creation-dod.md exists AND status == approved BEFORE Author agent activates
  compliance_check: Creation Orchestrator gate condition

R03:
  rule: >
    No policy may be approved without independent verification by a structurally
    separate Verifier agent against its approved DoD.
  test: review-verification.md exists AND verified_by != created_by
  compliance_check: Review Verifier; Verification Independence Policy

R04:
  rule: >
    No operational policy (Tiers 3–5) may contain a rule that contradicts any
    principles-derived policy (Tier 2).
  test: >
    Review Synthesizer lens-accuracy review explicitly checks for Tier 2
    contradictions; any contradiction is a mandatory rejection condition.
  compliance_check: Perspective Reviewer (lens-accuracy)

R05:
  rule: >
    No principles-derived policy (Tier 2) may be created, amended, or retired
    without documented evidence of systemic need drawn from a minimum of
    3 consecutive CI cycles.
  test: ci-synthesis.md references >= 3 cycles AND issue appears in all 3
  compliance_check: DLM SysAdmin Agent; Change Management Policy

R06:
  rule: >
    This policy (Tier 1) may only be amended with a change directive that cites
    quorum CI evidence across a minimum of 3 CI cycles and carries explicit
    SysAdmin approval.
  test: change-directive.md cites >= 3 CI cycle refs AND approved_by == sysadmin
  compliance_check: SysAdmin Verifier

R07:
  rule: >
    Every policy must declare all dependencies on other policies, standards,
    templates, and reference documents in its DEPENDENCIES block.
  test: dependencies[] is complete; Dependency Index Agent validates on indexing
  compliance_check: Indexing Verifier; Template Validator

R08:
  rule: >
    Circular dependencies between policies are forbidden.
  test: Dependency Index Agent finds no cycles in dependency-graph.md
  compliance_check: Dependency Index Agent on every Indexing stage

R09:
  rule: >
    All policies must specify a review schedule in their REVIEW_SCHEDULE section
    consistent with their tier's maximum review interval (see REVIEW_SCHEDULE).
  test: REVIEW_SCHEDULE section present AND interval <= tier maximum
  compliance_check: Template Validator; Quality Monitor (tracks overdue reviews)

R10:
  rule: >
    A policy that supersedes another must declare the superseded doc_id in its
    supersedes field. The superseded policy's superseded_by field must be updated
    at the same time.
  test: >
    supersedes != null AND referenced doc has superseded_by == this doc_id
    AND both are updated atomically by Version Control Agent
  compliance_check: Storage Verifier; Version Registry Updater

R11:
  rule: >
    Retired policies are archived and their status set to archived. No policy
    may be deleted from the DLMS.
  test: >
    No policy doc_id appears in the disposal queue.
    Archival stage is the terminal stage for policies.
  compliance_check: Status Transition Agent; Archival Verifier

R12:
  rule: >
    Policy changes must be initiated by a SysAdmin change directive. No agent
    may modify a policy without a corresponding approved directive.
  test: >
    Every policy in under_review status has a corresponding change-directive.md
    with decision: accepted
  compliance_check: Agent Behaviour Analyst; Audit Trail

R13:
  rule: >
    An approved exception to a Tier 3, 4, or 5 policy rule must be documented
    in a time-limited exception record. Exceptions to Tier 1 and Tier 2 policies
    do not exist.
  test: >
    Exception record exists AND expiry_at is set AND exception_scope is specific
    (not blanket). Tier 1 and 2 rules have no exception records.
  compliance_check: SysAdmin Verifier

R14:
  rule: >
    All policies must be assigned retention_class: RC-permanent.
  test: retention_class == RC-permanent
  compliance_check: Indexing Verifier; Metadata Standard Policy

R15:
  rule: >
    No policy may enter the Approved status if any policy it depends on
    (via is-governed-by or implements relationship) is in draft or
    under_review status.
  test: >
    All dependency docs with rel: is-governed-by OR rel: implements
    have status: approved
  compliance_check: Approval Gate Agent

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Applies to: Tier 3, 4, and 5 policies only.
Tier 1 and Tier 2 policies: no exceptions process exists.

STEPS:
  1. Requesting party submits an exception request document (TMPL-specification-001)
     specifying: policy doc_id, rule id, reason, duration, mitigating_controls[].
  2. DLM SysAdmin Agent reviews and issues a directive (accept/reject).
  3. SysAdmin Verifier independently verifies the directive.
  4. If accepted: exception record is created, indexed, and linked to the
     relevant policy via dependencies[].
  5. Exception expiry triggers a Distribution notification to SysAdmin.
  6. Expired exceptions are archived; they do not auto-renew.
  7. All exceptions are reviewed at the policy's next scheduled review cycle.

## ENFORCEMENT
MONITORING:
  - Agent Behaviour Analyst: monitors for policy rule violations in agent actions
  - Quality Monitor: monitors verification pass/fail rates per policy type
  - Audit Trail Agent: immutable record of all policy-related actions

VIOLATION_HANDLING:
  - Individual violation: logged in audit-trail.log; surfaced in
    agent-behaviour-report.md
  - Systemic violation (3+ occurrences in one CI cycle): triggers
    CI recommendation
  - Tier 2 violation: immediate escalation to SysAdmin per
    SysAdmin Escalation Policy
  - No violation may be resolved by retroactive policy modification

AUTHORITY:
  - DLM SysAdmin Agent is the sole authority for policy enforcement decisions
  - No executor agent has enforcement authority

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  tier_1:
    interval_max:  12 months
    trigger_conditions:
      - Systemic evidence from >= 3 CI cycles
      - Tier 1 rule breach confirmed by Audit Trail
  tier_2:
    interval_max:  12 months
    trigger_conditions:
      - Principle breach evidence confirmed by Audit Trail
      - CI recommendation citing multi-cycle pattern
  tier_3:
    interval_max:  6 months
    trigger_conditions:
      - CI recommendation
      - SLA breach pattern (>= 2 consecutive cycles)
  tier_4:
    interval_max:  6 months
    trigger_conditions:
      - System structural change
      - Impact assessment triggered by upstream change
  tier_5:
    interval_max:  3 months
    trigger_conditions:
      - SLA breach
      - CI performance report anomaly
