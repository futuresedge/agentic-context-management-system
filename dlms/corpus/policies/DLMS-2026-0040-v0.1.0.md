## SUMMARY
This policy governs the archival stage of the DLMS document lifecycle —
the preconditions that must be satisfied before a document transitions to
archived status, the agents with authority to execute archival, the access
control state of archived documents, and the audit requirements for all
archival events. The status transition itself is executed per Document
Status Transition Policy (DLMS-2026-0037). This is a Tier 3 Operational
Lifecycle policy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all DLMS corpus documents transitioning to archived status;
the Archival Stage Orchestrator (the agent that initiates and oversees
archival); the Retention Evaluator (which triggers archival upon retention
period elapse); the Status Transition Agent (which executes the status
change per DLMS-2026-0037); the Audit Trail Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
(for cold-archived document review authority).

Excludes: the deletion of documents after their cold-archive period
elapses (governed by Document Retention Policy DLMS-2026-0039 R07–R08);
the archival of stage artefacts and pipeline artefacts other than corpus
documents (those are governed by their respective templates and the
Retention Policy); interim archival within an active stage (documents
must reach approved or superseded status before archival is valid).

Bootstrap exception: DLMS-2026-0001 through DLMS-2026-0013 may be
archived under this policy once the approval pipeline is operational and
their status transitions to approved.

## DEFINITIONS

Archival Stage Orchestrator:
  The agent that coordinates the archival workflow: verifying preconditions,
  invoking the Archival DoD completion, co-ordinating the access control
  freeze, and issuing the transition directive to the Status Transition
  Agent. The Archival Stage Orchestrator does not write the status field
  directly — that is the Status Transition Agent's authority.

archival recommendation artefact:
  A structured artefact produced by the Archival Stage Orchestrator at
  the start of the archival workflow, documenting the reason for archival,
  the applicable retention class, and the post-archival state (cold or
  deleted at expiry). The artefact is a precondition for archival gate
  passage, not a recommendation subject to rejection — it is a record.

cold-archived state:
  The state of a document after an archive-cold action: the document
  file is retained in the corpus, its status remains archived, its
  context_eligible field is set to false, and it may not be included
  in any agent context package. Cold-archived documents are readable
  by sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin for audit purposes only.

Archival DoD:
  The Definition of Done artefact for the Archival stage, conforming
  to TMPL-dod-archival-001 (DLMS-2026-0029). Must be completed and
  verified before the archival status transition executes.

archival trigger:
  Either of the two conditions that initiate archival: (a) the
  Retention Evaluator signals that a document's retention period has
  elapsed, or (b) the document is superseded by a newer approved version.
  Both triggers are valid only for documents in approved or superseded
  status. No other archival trigger is valid.

## RULES

### Archival_Eligibility

R01:
  rule: >
    Only documents in approved or superseded status may be archived.
    A document in draft or under_review status may not be archived.
    The approved → archived and superseded → archived transitions are
    the only valid paths to archived status per DLMS-2026-0037 R02.
  test: >
    IF archival is initiated: document.status IN ['approved', 'superseded']
  checked_by: Archival Stage Orchestrator; Status Transition Agent

R02:
  rule: >
    Archival may only be triggered by one of two valid archival triggers:
    (a) the Retention Evaluator issues a retention-period-elapsed event
    for this doc_id, or (b) the document carries a non-null superseded_by
    field referencing an approved superseding document. Both triggers
    require the document to be in approved or superseded status.
  test: >
    archival trigger event IN ['retention-period-elapsed', 'supersession-complete']
    AND document.status IN ['approved', 'superseded']
  checked_by: Archival Stage Orchestrator; Retention Evaluator

### Archival_Preconditions

R03:
  rule: >
    An archival recommendation artefact must be produced by the Archival
    Stage Orchestrator before the Archival DoD is opened. The artefact
    must record: doc_id, archival trigger, retention_class,
    post-archival end-of-retention action per DLMS-2026-0022, and
    the projected cold-archive or deletion date where applicable.
  test: >
    An archival-recommendation artefact exists for this doc_id with
    all five required fields populated AND artefact timestamp precedes
    Archival DoD open timestamp.
  checked_by: Archival Stage Orchestrator; Stage Verifier

R04:
  rule: >
    The Archival DoD (conforming to DLMS-2026-0029) must be completed
    and independently verified before the archival status transition
    executes. Verification must be performed by an agent independent
    of the Archival Stage Orchestrator per DLMS-2026-0014.
  test: >
    archival-dod.status == 'complete' AND
    archival-dod.verified_by != archival-dod.created_by AND
    archival-dod.verified_by != 'agent:archival-stage-orchestrator'
  checked_by: Stage Verifier; Approval Gate Agent

R05:
  rule: >
    The Archival Stage Orchestrator must freeze the document's access
    controls before issuing the archival transition directive. Access
    control freeze means: no agent other than sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
    may write to the document file after archival. The freeze is recorded
    as an access-control-frozen audit event.
  test: >
    An access-control-frozen audit event exists for this doc_id AND
    event.timestamp < archival transition event.timestamp.
  checked_by: Archival Stage Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Post_Archival_State

R06:
  rule: >
    Upon archival, the context_eligible field of the archived document
    must be set to false. Archived documents must not be included in
    any agent context package regardless of their prior
    context_eligible value.
  test: >
    FOR all documents D where D.status == 'archived':
    D.context_eligible == false
  checked_by: Archival Stage Orchestrator; Context Delivery Layer;
              Agent Format Validator

R07:
  rule: >
    Archived documents are immutable. No agent may modify any field of
    a document in archived status. The Version Control Agent must not
    produce a new version of an archived document. If an archived
    document's content requires change, a new document superseding it
    must be created and follow the full creation and approval pipeline.
  test: >
    No write event in the audit trail targets a document with
    status: archived, except for the initial status transition itself.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

R08:
  rule: >
    The access controls on an archived document are preserved in the
    same state they were in at archival. No agent may modify access
    permissions on an archived document. sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin retains
    read access to all archived documents for audit and governance
    purposes.
  test: >
    No access-control-modification event exists in the audit trail
    for any document with status: archived after the
    access-control-frozen event timestamp.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Archival_Audit_Requirements

R09:
  rule: >
    An archival-complete audit event must be logged by the Audit Trail
    Agent upon successful archival stage completion. The event must
    include: doc_id, archival trigger, archival-recommendation artefact
    reference, Archival DoD artefact reference, status transition event
    reference, access-control-frozen event reference, and timestamp.
    All seven fields are mandatory.
  test: >
    An archival-complete event exists for this doc_id with all seven
    required fields populated.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

R10:
  rule: >
    Archival log entries are RC-permanent. The record of a document's
    archival must persist indefinitely in the audit trail regardless
    of the document's own retention class or subsequent deletion.
  test: >
    For all archival-complete audit events E:
    E.retention_class == 'RC-permanent'
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

### Context_Package_Exclusion

R11:
  rule: >
    The Context Delivery Layer must exclude all archived documents from
    context packages. An archived document's file path must not appear
    in the reads list of any context package assembled after the
    archival-complete event for that doc_id.
  test: >
    For all context packages P assembled after timestamp T:
    no item I in P.items where I.doc_id has an archival-complete
    event with timestamp < T.
  checked_by: Context Delivery Layer; Agent Behaviour Analyst

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Tier 3 exceptions are permitted on a time-limited basis with SysAdmin
approval per DLMS-2026-0001. Exception requests for this policy must
address precondition timing only (e.g. Archival DoD format variance in
a transitional pipeline state). No exception may waive R04 (DoD
completion required), R05 (access control freeze), R06 (context_eligible
set to false), R07 (post-archival immutability), R09 (archival audit
event), or R10 (archival log permanence).

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Archival Stage Orchestrator; Retention Evaluator
  gate_positions:
    - Pre-archival: Archival Stage Orchestrator validates R01–R05
      before issuing archival transition directive
    - Archival DoD: Stage Verifier validates Archival DoD completion
      (R04) at DoD gate
    - Post-archival: Agent Format Validator validates R06 (context_eligible
      false) immediately after status transition
    - CI cycle: Agent Behaviour Analyst audits R07 and R08 for all
      archived documents
  audit: >
    All archival recommendations are logged as archival-recommendation events.
    All Archival DoD completions are logged per DLMS-2026-0029.
    All access control freezes are logged as access-control-frozen events.
    All archival completions are logged as archival-complete events (RC-permanent).
    All context_eligible field changes are logged per DLMS-2026-0015.
  violation_type:   Tier 3 operational violation (R07, R08 violations
                    are escalated as Tier 2 structural violations)
  escalation: >
    R07 violations (write to archived document) and R11 violations
    (archived document in context package) are immediately escalated
    to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin as structural violations. All other
    violations are captured in the CI cycle report for SysAdmin review.

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      6 months (Tier 3 maximum)
  trigger_conditions:
    - The Document Status Transition Policy (DLMS-2026-0037) is amended
      in a way that changes archival transition preconditions
    - The Document Retention Policy (DLMS-2026-0039) is amended in a
      way that changes archive-cold or deletion timing
    - The Retention Schedule (DLMS-2026-0022) is updated with new
      classes affecting archival behaviour
    - A CI cycle identifies a systematic pattern of archived documents
      appearing in context packages
