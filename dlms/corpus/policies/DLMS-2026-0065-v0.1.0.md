## SUMMARY
This policy governs the creation, versioning, validation, and retirement of
all TMPL-* documents in the DLMS corpus, including the meta-template
(DLMS-2026-0002). It defines the obligations of Template Manager and Template
Validator, the versioning tier constraints that determine when SysAdmin
approval is required, and the grandfathering rule that protects documents
authored under prior template versions. This is a Tier 3 Operational Lifecycle
policy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all TMPL-* documents in the DLMS corpus — including Wave 2
content templates (DLMS-2026-0005 through 0013), Wave 6 foundational artefact
templates, Wave 9 stage artefact templates, and the meta-template
(DLMS-2026-0002); Template Manager; Template Validator; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
in the template approval and retirement authorisation context.

Excludes: the content quality of documents authored using templates (governed
by their respective doc_type policy and lifecycle policies); the frontmatter
schema of DLMS documents (governed by DLMS-2026-0004); the assignment of
taxonomy terms to documents (governed by DLMS-2026-0019 and DLMS-2026-0021);
Knowledge Base templates (governed by DLMS-2026-0063 and DLMS-2026-0064).

## DEFINITIONS

compatibility assessment:
  An artefact produced by Template Manager before a minor or major template
  version increment is published. It states which document classes are
  affected by the change and whether artefacts conformant under the
  previous template version remain conformant under the new version.
  Required per R05.

grandfathering:
  The principle that a document authored under a prior template_ver remains
  compliant under that template_ver. Re-validation against a newer version
  is not required unless triggered by the document's own review cycle or a
  SysAdmin directive. Formalised in R06.

propagation impact assessment:
  An artefact required before changes to the meta-template (DLMS-2026-0002).
  It enumerates all TMPL-* documents that implement DLMS-2026-0002, identifies
  the fields or structural conventions affected by the proposed change, and
  assesses whether existing templates remain conformant. Required per R09.

retirement:
  The act of marking a template version as superseded by recording a non-null
  superseded_by value referencing the successor version. A retired template
  version may not be used for new document authoring per R08, but existing
  documents authored under it are grandfathered per R06.

template-creation-proposal:
  An artefact submitted by any authorised agent requesting that a new TMPL-*
  document be created. The proposal must specify the proposed template_id,
  target doc_type, and rationale. Required before Template Manager begins
  authoring per R02.

## RULES

### Governed_Scope

R01:
  rule: >
    This policy applies to all TMPL-* documents in the DLMS corpus without
    exception. The meta-template (DLMS-2026-0002, TMPL-template-001) is
    subject to this policy with elevated change control obligations per R09.
    No TMPL-* document may be created, revised, or retired outside the
    process defined in this policy.
  test: >
    For any TMPL-* document lifecycle event: a corresponding template-lifecycle
    audit event (R12) exists. Absence of a template-lifecycle event for any
    TMPL-* document change is itself a policy violation.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Template Validator

### Template_Creation

R02:
  rule: >
    Any agent may submit a template-creation-proposal to the SysAdmin
    directive queue requesting that a new TMPL-* document be created.
    Template Manager may not begin authoring a new TMPL-* document without
    a SysAdmin directive approving the proposal. The directive must specify
    the approved template_id and target doc_type. Template IDs are assigned
    per Naming Convention Policy (DLMS-2026-0003).
  test: >
    For each newly created TMPL-* document: a SysAdmin directive exists in
    the audit log referencing the template-creation-proposal and specifying
    the approved template_id before the document's created_at timestamp.
    Absence of this directive means the document was created without
    authorisation.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin

R03:
  rule: >
    Every new TMPL-* document must be authored using the meta-template
    (DLMS-2026-0002, TMPL-template-001) as its structural basis — that is,
    DLMS-2026-0002 must be listed as a dependency with rel: implements in
    the new template's frontmatter. The new template must pass Template
    Validator review (R10) before entering the corpus at any status above
    draft.
  test: >
    For each TMPL-* document: dependencies includes an entry with doc_id:
    DLMS-2026-0002 and rel: implements AND a Template Validator review
    record exists before the document's status transitions beyond draft.
  checked_by: Template Validator; Approval Gate Agent

### Template_Versioning

R04:
  rule: >
    Template versioning follows Document Versioning Policy (DLMS-2026-0038)
    semver rules, with these additional tier constraints specific to
    TMPL-* documents:
    Patch (x.x.N+1): Non-semantic corrections only — typo fixes, field
      description clarifications that do not alter semantics. No changes to
      field names, field types, required/nullable status, enum values, or
      validation rules.
    Minor (x.N+1.0): Field additions (new required or optional fields may
      be added, but existing fields may not be removed or have their types
      changed); new validation rules that do not invalidate artefacts
      conformant under the previous version; description updates that
      clarify but do not alter field semantics.
    Major (N+1.0.0): Field removals; field type changes; new or modified
      validation rules that would invalidate artefacts that were conformant
      under the previous version. Major increments require SysAdmin approval
      and a compatibility assessment (R05) before publication.
  test: >
    For each template version increment: the change type is correctly
    classified against the tier constraints above. A patch that removes
    a field, or a minor that adds a rule invalidating prior artefacts, is
    a misclassified increment and a Tier 3 violation.
  checked_by: Template Validator; Approval Gate Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
              (for major increments)

R05:
  rule: >
    On any minor or major version increment to a TMPL-* document, Template
    Manager must produce a compatibility assessment before the new version
    is published. The assessment must state: (a) which document classes
    (doc_types) use this template and are therefore affected by the change;
    (b) whether artefacts conformant under the previous template version
    remain conformant under the new version; and (c) if not, what
    remediation is required for affected documents.
  test: >
    For each minor or major template version increment: a compatibility
    assessment artefact exists with a created_at timestamp preceding the
    new template version's publication event. Absence of the assessment
    blocks publication per Approval Gate Agent checks.
  checked_by: Template Validator; Approval Gate Agent

### Backward_Compatibility

R06:
  rule: >
    Documents authored under a prior template_ver remain compliant under
    their as-authored template_ver. The template_ver field in the document's
    frontmatter is the binding compliance reference for that document.
    Re-validation against a newer template version is not required unless
    triggered by the document's own scheduled review cycle as defined in
    its REVIEW_SCHEDULE or by an explicit SysAdmin directive.
  test: >
    For any document review triggered by the template versioning process:
    a SysAdmin directive exists authorising the re-validation review.
    Reviews of grandfathered documents without a directive or scheduled
    review trigger are unnecessary work and must not alter the document's
    status.
  checked_by: Approval Gate Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin

### Retirement

R07:
  rule: >
    A template version is retired only by supersession — a successor minor
    or major version is published, and the retiring template version's
    superseded_by field is updated to reference the successor. Retirement
    must be announced in the SysAdmin briefing queue at least one complete
    CI cycle before it takes effect. No template version may be deleted from
    the corpus; retired versions are retained with superseded_by non-null.
  test: >
    For any template version with a non-null superseded_by: a retirement
    announcement event exists in the SysAdmin briefing queue with a
    timestamp at least one CI cycle prior to the retirement event. Absence
    of this announcement is a process violation.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin

R08:
  rule: >
    After a template version is retired (superseded_by is non-null), no
    new DLMS document may be authored using that template version as its
    template_ver value. Existing documents authored under the retired
    version are grandfathered per R06 and are not affected by the
    retirement. Template Manager must enforce this gate at document-creation
    time by checking whether the requested template_ver is retired.
  test: >
    For each new document creation event: the template_ver referenced in
    the new document's frontmatter is not superseded (superseded_by is null
    in the referenced TMPL-* document at the time of creation). Documents
    created with a retired template_ver are non-conformant and must not be
    promoted beyond draft status.
  checked_by: Template Manager; Approval Gate Agent

### Meta_Template_Protection

R09:
  rule: >
    Changes to the meta-template (DLMS-2026-0002, TMPL-template-001) require:
    (a) a propagation impact assessment enumerating all TMPL-* documents that
    declare rel: implements against DLMS-2026-0002, identifying which fields
    or structural conventions are affected; (b) explicit SysAdmin directive
    approving the change, citing the propagation impact assessment by artefact
    ID. The propagation impact assessment must be completed before the SysAdmin
    directive request is submitted. No change to DLMS-2026-0002 may be
    published without both artefacts in the audit record.
  test: >
    For each version increment of DLMS-2026-0002: both a propagation impact
    assessment artefact and a SysAdmin directive exist with timestamps
    preceding the new version's publication event. Absence of either artefact
    voids the version increment.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin

### Validation_Gate

R10:
  rule: >
    Every new or revised TMPL-* document must pass Template Validator review
    before publication to any status above draft. Template Validator checks:
    (a) structural conformance with DLMS-2026-0002 — the document follows the
    meta-template's TARGET_DOC_TYPE, SCHEMA, and VALIDATION_RULES conventions;
    (b) that all field names in the SCHEMA block match terms registered in the
    DLMS controlled vocabulary (DLMS-2026-0021) or are proposed for
    registration per Taxonomy Policy (DLMS-2026-0019).
  test: >
    For each TMPL-* document transition beyond draft: a Template Validator
    review record exists with verdict: pass before the transition event.
    A review record with verdict: fail blocks the transition; the Template
    Manager must produce a corrected version.
  checked_by: Template Validator; Approval Gate Agent

### Context_Boundary

R11:
  rule: >
    Template Manager's reads list during an authoring task is confined to:
    (a) the approved template-creation-proposal; and (b) DLMS-2026-0002
    (TMPL-template-001). Template Validator's reads list during a validation
    task is confined to: (a) the TMPL-* document under validation; and
    (b) DLMS-2026-0002. Neither agent may expand this read set during a
    single task. Compatibility assessments (R05) and propagation impact
    assessments (R09) are produced by Template Manager in a separate
    designated assessment task with a broader read set specified in the
    task assignment, not during the authoring task itself.
  test: >
    All reads by Template Manager during an authoring task and by Template
    Validator during a validation task are confined to the sets specified
    above. Any read outside these sets during the specified task types is
    a context boundary violation per DLMS-2026-0016.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Audit

R12:
  rule: >
    All template lifecycle events must produce a template-lifecycle audit
    event. The event must contain: template_id (the TMPL-xxx-NNN format
    identifier), doc_id, former_version (null for new templates),
    new_version, lifecycle_event (created | version-incremented | retired),
    and initiating_agent. Template-creation-proposal submissions and
    SysAdmin directive approvals are also separately logged per
    DLMS-2026-0015.
  test: >
    For each TMPL-* document lifecycle change: a template-lifecycle audit
    event with all six required fields exists before any downstream agent
    acts on the changed template. Absence of this event is a Tier 3 audit
    failure per DLMS-2026-0015.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Quality Monitor

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Tier 3 exceptions are permitted on a time-limited basis with SysAdmin
approval. To request an exception to any rule in this policy: (1) The
requesting agent produces a policy-exception-request artefact addressed
to the SysAdmin directive queue, citing the rule reference, the reason
the rule cannot be satisfied, and proposed mitigating controls. (2)
sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin reviews and, if approved, issues a
policy-exception-grant directive specifying the rule, scope, expiry date,
and audit conditions. (3) The exception is logged as a policy-exception
audit event visible in the next CI cycle report. No exception may extend
beyond 90 days without renewal. No exception may waive R09 (meta-template
protection). Exceptions to R04 major version constraints must specifically
address the compatibility assessment requirement.

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Template Manager; Template Validator; Approval Gate Agent
  gate_positions:
    - Per-template at creation: Template Validator runs R10 before any
      transition above draft is permitted
    - Per-template at version increment: Template Manager produces
      compatibility assessment (R05) before publication; Approval Gate
      Agent validates R04 classification before approving
    - Per-meta-template change: sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin validates R09
      before approving any DLMS-2026-0002 version increment
    - CI cycle: Quality Monitor reviews template-lifecycle events for
      compliance with R12 and flags unauthorised template changes
  audit: >
    All template-lifecycle events are logged per R12. Context boundary
    violations by Template Manager or Template Validator (R11 breach)
    are logged as context-boundary-violation events per DLMS-2026-0015.
    Unauthorised template changes (no template-lifecycle event present)
    trigger an integrity-violation event requiring SysAdmin review.
  violation_type:   Tier 3 operational violation; R11 breaches are also
                    Tier 2 context boundary violations per DLMS-2026-0016
  escalation: >
    Unauthorised changes to DLMS-2026-0002 without a propagation impact
    assessment and SysAdmin directive (R09 breach) are immediately
    escalated to the SysAdmin directive queue per Policy Governance Policy
    (DLMS-2026-0001). Systematic validation failures — same structural
    defect appearing across multiple TMPL-* documents in a single CI
    cycle — trigger a SysAdmin review of the meta-template itself.

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      6 months (Tier 3 maximum)
  trigger_conditions:
    - The meta-template (DLMS-2026-0002) is revised in a way that changes
      the TARGET_DOC_TYPE convention, SCHEMA field structure, or
      VALIDATION_RULES expectations for all implementing templates —
      mandatory trigger; R03 and R10 must be updated accordingly.
    - Document Versioning Policy (DLMS-2026-0038) is amended in a way
      that changes semver rules — the tier constraint table in R04 must
      be reviewed for consistency with the updated versioning rules.
    - The DLMS controlled vocabulary (DLMS-2026-0021) is expanded to
      include new term categories that affect field naming conventions
      checked by Template Validator under R10.
    - CI cycle reports reveal a pattern of unauthorised or incorrectly
      classified template version increments, suggesting the tier
      constraint definitions in R04 are ambiguous.
