## SUMMARY
This SLA governs the performance of the DLMS distribution stage, defining
measurable latency targets for distribution plan production, distribution
execution, per-recipient delivery confirmation, and DoD verification. It
establishes the binding performance expectations for the Distribution
Orchestrator and the associated measurement and breach response process.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all DLMS distribution operations for documents that have
passed the distribution stage DoD gate; the Distribution Orchestrator
agent; the DoD Verifier (distribution stage); all distribution events
for all confidentiality classes.

Excludes: creation, review, storage, and archival stage performance;
document content or metadata quality assessment (DLMS-2026-0043); access
control enforcement at read/write time (DLMS-2026-0044); network or
external delivery infrastructure reliability (outside DLMS control).

Boundary conditions:
  M01 clock starts:  distribution DoD passed audit event for the
                     doc_id and doc_version (per DLMS-2026-0028)
  M01 clock stops:   planned_at in the Distribution Plan artefact
                     (DLMS-2026-0053) for that doc_id and doc_version
  M02 clock starts:  started_at in the Distribution Log artefact
                     (DLMS-2026-0054)
  M02 clock stops:   completed_at in the same Distribution Log artefact
  M03 clock starts:  started_at in the Distribution Log artefact
  M03 clock stops:   delivered_at per recipient entry in
                     distribution_outcomes; M03 applies only to
                     recipient entries with status: delivered
  M04 clock starts:  Distribution DoD record submission audit event
  M04 clock stops:   DoD Verifier produces verified Distribution DoD
                     record (template DLMS-2026-0028)

## DEFINITIONS

breach:
  A metric value that equals or exceeds breach_threshold for that metric.
  A breach triggers the breach-level BREACH_RESPONSE and must be logged
  as an sla-breach audit event referencing the affected doc_id.

distribution event:
  A single execution of the distribution process for a specific doc_id,
  doc_version, and Distribution Plan, resulting in a Distribution Log
  artefact. A redistribution event is a separate distribution event
  with a new Distribution Log, referencing a new or the original plan.

elapsed time:
  Clock time in hours between two defined events per the SCOPE Boundary
  conditions above. Measured from auditable timestamps in DLMS artefacts
  or audit log events only.

warning:
  A metric value exceeding warning_threshold but below breach_threshold.
  Logged and stage orchestrator notified; stage advancement not blocked.

## METRICS

M01:
  name:                 Distribution plan production latency
  description:          Elapsed time from the distribution DoD pass event
                        to the Distribution Orchestrator producing a
                        Distribution Plan artefact. Measures the
                        pre-distribution planning speed.
  unit:                 hours
  target:               <= 2h
  warning_threshold:    > 90 minutes
  breach_threshold:     > 4h
  measurement_source:   distribution-dod-passed audit event;
                        planned_at in Distribution Plan artefact
                        (DLMS-2026-0053)
  measurement_frequency: per-distribution-event

M02:
  name:                 Distribution execution duration
  description:          Elapsed time from the start of a distribution
                        event to its completion (regardless of outcome
                        per recipient). Measures the total execution
                        window of the Distribution Orchestrator.
  unit:                 hours
  target:               <= 2h
  warning_threshold:    > 90 minutes
  breach_threshold:     > 4h
  measurement_source:   started_at and completed_at in Distribution Log
                        artefact (DLMS-2026-0054)
  measurement_frequency: per-distribution-event

M03:
  name:                 Per-recipient delivery confirmation latency
  description:          Elapsed time from the start of a distribution
                        event to delivery confirmation per recipient,
                        for recipients achieving status: delivered.
                        Measures the speed of end-to-end delivery for
                        individual recipients within a distribution event.
  unit:                 hours
  target:               <= 4h
  warning_threshold:    > 3h
  breach_threshold:     > 8h
  measurement_source:   started_at in Distribution Log; delivered_at
                        per recipient in distribution_outcomes where
                        status is delivered (DLMS-2026-0054)
  measurement_frequency: per-distribution-event per-recipient
                         (delivered recipients only)

M04:
  name:                 Distribution DoD verification latency
  description:          Elapsed time from Distribution DoD record
                        submission to the DoD Verifier producing a
                        verified Distribution DoD record. Measures the
                        DoD verification gate speed for the distribution
                        stage. Formalises the 24h provisional DoD
                        threshold in DLMS-2026-0043 R09.
  unit:                 hours
  target:               <= 24h
  warning_threshold:    > 18h
  breach_threshold:     > 48h
  measurement_source:   Distribution DoD record submission audit event;
                        DoD Verifier verified-dod-produced audit event
                        (template DLMS-2026-0028)
  measurement_frequency: per-document

## MEASUREMENT_METHOD

collection_agent:    Distribution Stage Metrics Collector — collects raw
                     timestamps from Distribution Plan artefacts,
                     Distribution Log artefacts, DoD records, and
                     associated audit events per doc_id and distribution
                     event.

aggregation_agent:   Metrics Aggregator — receives raw per-event
                     measurements from Distribution Stage Metrics
                     Collector and produces aggregated metric values
                     (median, 95th percentile, breach rate, delivery
                     success rate) per CI cycle. Must differ from
                     collection_agent per Verification Independence
                     Policy (DLMS-2026-0014).

reporting_artefact:  TMPL-performance-report-001 (Wave 12, planned —
                     see NOTES). Until Wave 12 is complete, performance
                     data is reported through interim ad hoc report
                     artefacts maintained by the Metrics Aggregator.

reporting_frequency: per CI cycle

baseline_ref:        Provisional design-intent baseline established at
                     system activation; target values in this SLA are
                     the baseline. After 90 days of live operation the
                     Metrics Aggregator establishes an operational
                     baseline for the Anomaly Detector.

## BREACH_RESPONSE

warning:
  trigger:               Any distribution stage metric value exceeds its
                         warning_threshold for a given distribution event
                         or doc_id.
  notification_targets:  [Distribution Stage Metrics Collector,
                          Distribution Stage Orchestrator]
  response_actions:
    - Distribution Stage Metrics Collector logs a time-warning audit
      event with metric_id, elapsed_time, threshold_value, and doc_id.
    - Distribution Stage Orchestrator monitors the in-flight event
      for risk of breach escalation.
  resolution_sla:        24h

breach:
  trigger:               Any distribution stage metric value equals or
                         exceeds its breach_threshold for a given
                         distribution event or doc_id.
  notification_targets:  [Distribution Stage Metrics Collector,
                          Distribution Stage Orchestrator,
                          agent:sla-manager]
  response_actions:
    - Distribution Stage Metrics Collector logs an sla-breach audit
      event with metric_id, elapsed_time, breach_threshold, and doc_id.
    - Distribution Stage Orchestrator logs a stage-escalation event
      and reviews recipient delivery outcomes for the affected event.
    - agent:sla-manager acknowledges breach and initiates review per
      SLA Breach Response Policy (Wave 13, planned).
  resolution_sla:        8h

critical:
  trigger:               M02 breach_threshold (> 4h) exceeded for three
                         or more distribution events in the same CI
                         cycle; or any distribution event where
                         completed_at cannot be recorded (event hangs
                         indefinitely without a completion timestamp).
  notification_targets:  [agent:sla-manager,
                          sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin]
  response_actions:
    - agent:sla-manager produces a performance anomaly report with
      root cause analysis for the affected metric(s) and events.
    - sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin reviews and issues a corrective directive
      within the resolution_sla window, including an abort-and-retry
      directive if an event is hanging.
  resolution_sla:        2h

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      3 months
  trigger_conditions:
    - Sustained M02 or M03 sla-breach events across two consecutive
      CI cycles indicate systemic distribution execution latency
      requiring SLA review — mandatory trigger.
    - DLMS-2026-0044 (Access Control Policy) is amended in a way that
      changes pre-distribution access verification requirements.
    - Distribution stage artefact templates DLMS-2026-0053 or
      DLMS-2026-0054 are amended in ways that affect timestamp fields
      used as measurement sources.
    - The definition of permitted distribution channels changes in a
      way that affects expected delivery latencies.

## NOTES
reporting_artefact forward reference: TMPL-performance-report-001 is
planned for Wave 12 and does not yet exist in the corpus. Until Wave 12
is complete, performance reporting is conducted through interim ad hoc
report artefacts. This SLA must be reviewed after Wave 12 is drafted to
update the reporting_artefact reference in MEASUREMENT_METHOD.

M03 measurement scope: M03 measures only recipients with final status
delivered. Recipients with status failed or pending at completed_at do
not contribute to M03 measurement. Failed deliveries are tracked as a
separate operational metric (delivery failure rate) — not defined in
this SLA version but flagged as a candidate for a future minor version.
