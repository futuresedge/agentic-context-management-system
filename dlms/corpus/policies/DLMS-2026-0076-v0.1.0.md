## SUMMARY
This policy defines the structure, timing, sequencing, and completion
criteria for a CI cycle — the recurring automated pipeline that monitors,
synthesises, and acts on continuous improvement signals across the DLMS.
Every CI cycle runs on a fixed 7-day schedule and produces a mandated
chain of artefacts terminating in a change directive issued by the DLM
SysAdmin.

## SCOPE

Applies to:
- All CI cycle artefacts: quality reports (QR-), performance reports
  (PR-), anomaly reports (AR-), bottleneck reports (BNR-), agent
  behaviour reports (ABR-), CI synthesis artefacts (CIS-), improvement
  recommendations (IMP-), SysAdmin briefings (SBR-), and change
  directives (CD-)
- All agents operating within the CI pipeline:
  agent:quality-monitor, agent:metrics-aggregator,
  agent:anomaly-detector, agent:bottleneck-analyst,
  agent:agent-behaviour-analyst, agent:ci-perspective-synthesizer,
  agent:recommendation-generator, agent:sysadmin-briefing-agent,
  agent:dlm-sysadmin-agent, agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator,
  agent:learning-synthesizer
- The DLM SysAdmin (sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin) in their change directive
  issuance role

Excludes:
- Individual stage pipeline execution (governed by stage SLA policies
  DLMS-2026-0057 through DLMS-2026-0061)
- Knowledge base contribution (governed by DLMS-2026-0063)
- Learning synthesis scheduling (governed by DLMS-2026-0064)

## DEFINITIONS

CI cycle:
  A structured, time-bounded monitoring and improvement process that
  collects data across all DLMS operational stages, synthesises findings
  from multiple perspectives, and produces a set of directed improvement
  recommendations that the DLM SysAdmin accepts, defers, or rejects.
  Each CI cycle is identified by a unique cycle_id (CYCLE-{YYYY}-{NNNN}).

cycle_id:
  The unique identifier for a CI cycle. Format: CYCLE-{YYYY}-{NNNN}
  where YYYY is the year and NNNN is a zero-padded sequential integer
  scoped to the year, starting at CYCLE-{YYYY}-0001.

monitor reports:
  The five artefacts produced in parallel by specialist CI monitor agents
  at the start of each cycle: quality report (QR-), performance report
  (PR-), anomaly report (AR-), bottleneck report (BNR-), and agent
  behaviour report (ABR-). All five must be complete before the synthesis
  chain begins.

synthesis chain:
  The ordered sequence of CI artefacts produced after all five monitor
  reports are available: CI synthesis (CIS-) → improvement
  recommendations (IMP-) → SysAdmin briefing (SBR-) → change directive
  (CD-). Each artefact depends on the preceding one.

cycle window:
  The 7-day calendar period in which all monitor reports and the full
  synthesis chain must be produced and a change directive issued.

cycle breach:
  A condition in which a CI cycle has not produced a change directive
  within 2 days of the SysAdmin briefing being issued, or in which a
  monitor report or synthesis chain artefact has been blocked for more
  than 24 hours without a logged escalation.

## RULES

### Scheduling

R01:
  rule: >
    A CI cycle must run on a fixed 7-day schedule. The cycle start day
    is set at DLMS operational bootstrap and must not change without a
    formal change directive (CD-) authorising the schedule change. Each
    cycle receives a unique cycle_id in the format CYCLE-{YYYY}-{NNNN},
    assigned sequentially within the year.
  test: >
    new_cycle.start_date - prior_cycle.start_date == 7 days AND
    new_cycle.cycle_id == increment(prior_cycle.cycle_id)
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R02:
  rule: >
    A new CI cycle must not begin before the prior cycle's change
    directive (CD-) has been issued. If the prior cycle's change
    directive is not available by the scheduled new cycle start time,
    agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator must log a cycle breach event and notify
    sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin before initiating the new cycle.
  test: >
    prior_cycle.change_directive_ref != null AND
    prior_cycle.change_directive_ref matches ^CD-\d{4}-\d{4}$
    BEFORE new_cycle.start is triggered.
    IF prior_cycle.change_directive_ref == null at scheduled start:
      cycle_breach event logged AND sysadmin notified
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Monitor Reports

R03:
  rule: >
    All five monitor reports — quality report (QR-), performance report
    (PR-), anomaly report (AR-), bottleneck report (BNR-), and agent
    behaviour report (ABR-) — must be produced within the first 48 hours
    of the cycle window. No synthesis chain artefact may be initiated
    until all five monitor reports are available.
  test: >
    EXISTS QR- for cycle_id AND
    EXISTS PR- for cycle_id AND
    EXISTS AR- for cycle_id AND
    EXISTS BNR- for cycle_id AND
    EXISTS ABR- for cycle_id
    BEFORE CIS- is initiated.
    Each report timestamp <= cycle_start + 48h.
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Agent Format Validator

R04:
  rule: >
    Monitor reports are produced independently and in parallel. No
    monitor agent may read the output of another monitor agent before
    producing its own report. agent:anomaly-detector is an exception and
    may read QR- and PR- artefacts as defined in DLMS-2026-0068, but
    must not read ABR- or BNR- artefacts.
  test: >
    agent:quality-monitor context does not include PR-, AR-, BNR-, or
    ABR- artefacts at time of QR- production. Agent context boundaries
    per DLMS-2026-0016.
  checked_by: agent:agent-behaviour-analyst; Agent Context Boundary Policy (DLMS-2026-0016)

### Synthesis Chain

R05:
  rule: >
    The synthesis chain must execute in strict sequence: all five monitor
    reports must be complete before CI synthesis (CIS-) begins;
    recommendations (IMP-) may not begin until CIS- is complete;
    SysAdmin briefing (SBR-) may not begin until IMP- is complete;
    change directive (CD-) may not be issued until SBR- is complete.
    No step in the chain may be skipped.
  test: >
    CIS-.generated_at > all([QR-.generated_at, PR-.generated_at,
      AR-.generated_at, BNR-.generated_at, ABR-.generated_at]) AND
    IMP-.generated_at > CIS-.generated_at AND
    SBR-.generated_at > IMP-.generated_at AND
    CD-.issued_at > SBR-.generated_at
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

R06:
  rule: >
    escalation_carried_forward must be honoured through the full synthesis
    chain. If any monitor report has escalation_required: true, the CI
    synthesis artefact must set escalation_carried_forward: true (per
    DLMS-2026-0071 V05). No agent in the synthesis chain may suppress,
    reset, or ignore an escalation signal passed from an upstream artefact.
  test: >
    IF any source_report.escalation_required == true
    THEN CIS-.escalation_carried_forward == true AND
         IMP-.escalation_required == true AND
         SBR-.escalation_required == true
  checked_by: Agent Format Validator; agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

### Completion and Breach

R07:
  rule: >
    A CI cycle is complete when a change directive (CD-) for that
    cycle_id has been issued by sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin or
    agent:dlm-sysadmin-agent. If no change directive is issued within
    2 days of the SysAdmin briefing issuance, a cycle breach event must
    be logged and sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin must be notified.
  test: >
    IF (now - SBR-.generated_at) > 48h AND CD-.issued_at == null
    THEN cycle_breach event logged AND sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin notified
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R08:
  rule: >
    If any CI chain artefact (monitor report or synthesis chain step) is
    blocked or unavailable for more than 24 hours after its scheduled
    production time, agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator must log a cycle breach
    event, record the blocking reason, and notify sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin.
  test: >
    FOR each expected artefact in cycle:
      IF artefact.available_at - artefact.scheduled_at > 24h
      THEN cycle_breach event logged with blocking_reason AND
           sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin notified
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

### Verification Independence

R09:
  rule: >
    Every CI chain artefact must be verified by a different agent than
    the one that generated it, in accordance with Verification
    Independence Policy (DLMS-2026-0014). agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator
    acts as the verification gate and must confirm the generated_by ≠
    verified_by condition for each artefact before advancing the chain.
  test: >
    FOR each artefact in CI chain:
      artefact.generated_by != artefact.verified_by
  checked_by: agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator; Agent Format Validator

### Retention

R10:
  rule: >
    All CI cycle artefacts must be retained in accordance with Document
    Retention Policy (DLMS-2026-0039). CI artefacts are operationally
    significant records; they must not be deleted, archived prematurely,
    or made inaccessible while the cycle they belong to has unresolved
    escalations or accepted directives pending implementation
    confirmation.
  test: >
    FOR each artefact in cycle:
      artefact.retention_class == RC-permanent OR
      artefact.status NOT archived WHILE
        cycle.unresolved_escalations > 0 OR
        cycle.pending_directive_confirmations > 0
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator; agent:ci-cycle-orchestrator

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS

Exceptions to this policy require Tier 3 exception approval:
1. An exception request must be submitted to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin as
   a logged audit event with the specific rule being excepted, the
   business reason, and the proposed compensating control.
2. sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin must approve within 24 hours or the default
   rule applies.
3. Approved exceptions are recorded in the change directive for the
   cycle in which the exception applies.
4. No exception may persist beyond a single CI cycle without a new
   approval.

The following cannot be excepted under any circumstances:
- R06 (escalation propagation) — Tier 2 constraint per DLMS-2026-0014
- R09 (verification independence) — Tier 2 constraint per DLMS-2026-0014

  - Before SBR- generation (IMP- complete — R05)
  - Before CD- issuance (SBR- complete — R05)
  - At cycle completion (CD- issued for cycle_id — R07)

                  sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin notified; cycle may not advance
                  past breach point without explicit SysAdmin acknowledgement.
