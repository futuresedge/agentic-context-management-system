## SUMMARY
This policy governs how long DLMS corpus documents and pipeline artefacts
are retained, which agents may trigger end-of-retention actions, and
the structural requirements that protect irreversible deletion behind a
mandatory human gate. It operationalises the Retention Schedule
(DLMS-2026-0022) for the DLMS pipeline. This is a Tier 3 Operational
Lifecycle policy.

## SCOPE
Applies to: all DLMS corpus documents; all pipeline artefacts produced
within the DLMS stages (stage artefacts, verification records, DoD
artefacts, audit events, classification recommendations); the
retention_class field in every document's frontmatter; the Retention
Evaluator agent; the Archival Stage Orchestrator (for archive-cold
actions); sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin (for human-gated deletion authority).

Excludes: documents held in external systems that receive DLMS context
packages as consumers but are not stored in the DLMS corpus; ephemeral
runtime agent state that is never persisted to the DLMS file system;
audit trail entries produced by the Audit Trail Agent (those are
governed by this policy's RC-permanent rule but their content is
governed by the Audit Trail Policy DLMS-2026-0015).

Bootstrap exception: the four Wave-1 bootstrap documents carry
RC-permanent and are subject to this policy's RC-permanent rules.
No end-of-retention action applies to them.

## DEFINITIONS

archive-cold action:
  An end-of-retention action that moves a document to cold storage —
  a read-accessible but non-context-eligible state — for a further
  retention period before permanent deletion. Cold-archived documents
  have context_eligible: false and may not be included in any agent
  context package. Access controls are preserved.

delete action:
  The permanent, irrecoverable removal of a document or artefact from
  the DLMS file system. Deletion is irreversible. Every deletion is
  preceded by a human gate confirmation from sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
  and is recorded as a permanent audit event regardless of the deleted
  document's own retention class.

end-of-retention action:
  The action taken by the Retention Evaluator when a document's
  retention period expires. Three actions are defined: retain (no action,
  RC-permanent only), archive-cold (RC-1 through RC-3), and delete
  (RC-4 through RC-7 after archive-cold period). Defined authoritatively
  in DLMS-2026-0022.

human deletion gate:
  The mandatory approval step at which sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin confirms
  a pending deletion before the Retention Evaluator executes the delete
  action. The gate is structural — the Retention Evaluator does not hold
  a delete tool for documents in classes RC-permanent, RC-1, RC-2,
  or RC-3 without an active human-confirmation event.

retain action:
  The end-of-retention action for RC-permanent documents. No deletion
  or cold-archival occurs. These documents persist indefinitely unless
  superseded and archived through the standard lifecycle pipeline.

Retention Evaluator:
  The sole agent authorised to trigger end-of-retention actions. The
  Retention Evaluator reads a document's retention_class from its
  frontmatter, consults DLMS-2026-0022 to determine the action and
  period, and generates a retention-evaluation event. It does not
  independently delete or archive — it generates directives that are
  either automatically executed (schedule-based) or gate-confirmed
  (deletion confirmation).

retention period:
  The duration a document must be kept from its created_at date before
  an end-of-retention action becomes eligible. Defined per retention
  class in DLMS-2026-0022. The retention period begins at created_at
  and does not restart on version increment.

## RULES

### Retention_Class_Assignment

R01:
  rule: >
    Every DLMS corpus document must carry a retention_class value drawn
    from the eight classes defined in DLMS-2026-0022: RC-permanent,
    RC-1, RC-2, RC-3, RC-4, RC-5, RC-6, or RC-7. Any other value
    is invalid.
  test: >
    retention_class IN ['RC-permanent', 'RC-1', 'RC-2', 'RC-3',
    'RC-4', 'RC-5', 'RC-6', 'RC-7']
  checked_by: Agent Format Validator; Retention Evaluator

R02:
  rule: >
    The following document types must be assigned RC-permanent and no
    other retention class is valid for them: policy, standard, taxonomy,
    specification, template. These document types are permanent by design
    — they constitute the governance corpus of the DLMS.
  test: >
    IF doc_type IN ['policy', 'standard', 'taxonomy', 'specification',
    'template']: retention_class == 'RC-permanent'
  checked_by: Agent Format Validator; Retention Evaluator

R03:
  rule: >
    All audit trail entries produced by the Audit Trail Agent are
    RC-permanent. This applies regardless of the retention class of the
    document or artefact whose events they record. Audit event permanence
    is non-negotiable and may not be varied by exception.
  test: >
    For all audit trail entries E: E.retention_class == 'RC-permanent'
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Retention_Period_Enforcement

R04:
  rule: >
    The Retention Evaluator is the sole agent authorised to trigger
    end-of-retention actions. No other agent or role may initiate
    archive-cold or delete actions outside of the retention evaluation
    process. The Retention Evaluator acts only when a document's
    retention period has elapsed as defined in DLMS-2026-0022.
  test: >
    For all archive-cold and delete events in the audit trail:
    event.actor == 'agent:retention-evaluator' AND
    a retention-period-elapsed event exists for this doc_id.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent; Agent Behaviour Analyst

R05:
  rule: >
    A document's retention period begins at its created_at timestamp.
    Version increments do not restart the retention period. No document
    may be deleted before its full retention period has elapsed.
  test: >
    retention_expiry_date = created_at + retention_period_for(retention_class)
    IF current_date < retention_expiry_date: delete action is invalid.
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator

### End_of_Retention_Actions

R06:
  rule: >
    RC-permanent documents must never receive a delete or archive-cold
    action. The end-of-retention action for RC-permanent is: retain
    (no action). The Retention Evaluator must not generate a deletion
    directive for any document with retention_class: RC-permanent.
  test: >
    No delete-directive or archive-cold-directive events exist in the
    audit trail for any document with retention_class == 'RC-permanent'.
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator; Audit Trail Agent; CI Monitor Agent

R07:
  rule: >
    Documents in retention classes RC-1, RC-2, and RC-3 must receive
    an archive-cold action when their primary retention period elapses.
    After the cold-archive period defined in DLMS-2026-0022 has elapsed,
    a deletion directive is generated, subject to the human deletion gate
    at R08. These documents must not be deleted without the cold-archive
    intermediate step.
  test: >
    For documents with retention_class IN ['RC-1', 'RC-2', 'RC-3']:
    IF status == 'archived': archive-cold event exists AND
    time(archive-cold event) >= created_at + primary_period.
    IF delete action: archive-cold event precedes delete event.
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator; Archival Stage Orchestrator

R08:
  rule: >
    All delete actions — for any retention class — require a human
    deletion gate confirmation from sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin before the
    delete action executes. The Retention Evaluator generates a
    deletion-pending event; deletion executes only after a
    deletion-confirmed event is logged by sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin.
    No agent holds an autonomous delete capability for corpus documents.
  test: >
    For all delete events E: a deletion-confirmed event exists with
    actor == 'sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin' AND
    deletion-confirmed.timestamp < E.timestamp AND
    deletion-confirmed.doc_id == E.doc_id.
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator; Audit Trail Agent

R09:
  rule: >
    Documents in retention classes RC-4 through RC-7 may be deleted
    at retention period expiry (subject to the human gate at R08).
    No intermediate cold-archive step is required. The delete action
    for these classes is direct after expiry.
  test: >
    For documents with retention_class IN ['RC-4', 'RC-5', 'RC-6', 'RC-7']:
    after retention period elapsed AND deletion-confirmed event exists:
    delete action is valid.
  checked_by: Retention Evaluator

### Deletion_Audit_Permanence

R10:
  rule: >
    The deletion audit event for any deleted document is itself
    RC-permanent. The deletion record must persist indefinitely in the
    audit trail regardless of the deleted document's own retention class.
    A corpus in which a document was deleted must always contain a
    permanent record that the deletion occurred, who confirmed it,
    and when.
  test: >
    For all deletion audit events E: E.retention_class == 'RC-permanent'
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

R11:
  rule: >
    Deletion audit events must include: doc_id, doc_type, retention_class,
    deletion actor (agent:retention-evaluator), confirmation actor
    (sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin), deletion-confirmed event reference,
    deletion timestamp, and reason. All seven fields are mandatory.
  test: >
    For all deletion audit events: all seven required fields are
    non-null.
  checked_by: Audit Trail Agent

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
Tier 3 exceptions are permitted on a time-limited basis with SysAdmin
approval per DLMS-2026-0001. To request an exception to retention period
timing (e.g. early deletion for legal reasons): the requesting party
presents a policy-exception-request artefact to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin
citing the legal or regulatory basis for early deletion. No exception
may waive R03 (audit event permanence), R06 (RC-permanent no-delete),
R08 (human deletion gate), or R10–R11 (deletion audit requirements).
Early deletion exceptions must themselves be permanently logged.

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Retention Evaluator; Audit Trail Agent
  gate_positions:
    - Retention evaluation cycle: Retention Evaluator checks all
      corpus documents for elapsed retention periods per defined schedule
    - Pre-deletion: Retention Evaluator validates R04–R09 before
      generating any deletion directive
    - Human gate: sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin issues deletion-confirmed
      event before any delete action executes
  audit: >
    All retention evaluations are logged as retention-evaluation events.
    All archive-cold actions are logged as archive-cold events.
    All deletion directives are logged as deletion-pending events.
    All deletion confirmations are logged by sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin.
    All delete actions are logged as deletion events (RC-permanent).
  violation_type:   Tier 3 operational violation (R06, R08 violations
                    are escalated as Tier 2 structural violations)
  escalation: >
    R06 violations (RC-permanent deletion attempt) and R08 violations
    (deletion without human gate) are immediately escalated to
    sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin as structural violations and blocked.
    All other violations are captured in the CI cycle report.

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      6 months (Tier 3 maximum)
  trigger_conditions:
    - The Retention Schedule (DLMS-2026-0022) is updated with new
      classes or amended periods
    - A regulatory or legal requirement changes the minimum retention
      period for any document type present in the corpus
    - A deletion audit event reveals a gap in the human gate process
    - The CI cycle identifies systematic retention class
      misassignment across newly created documents
