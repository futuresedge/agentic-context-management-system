## SUMMARY
This policy governs the multi-perspective review stage of the DLMS
document lifecycle. Every document must pass through a structured review
in which independent Perspective Reviewer agents evaluate the document
from distinct, non-overlapping analytical lenses before it may advance
to approval. No single reviewer's assessment is sufficient for approval.
This is a Tier 2 policy directly enforcing Principle 6
(Multi-Perspective Review).

## SCOPE
Applies to: all DLMS documents passing through the Review stage; all
Perspective Reviewer agents; the Review Orchestrator that coordinates
the review stage; the Review Synthesizer that consolidates reviewer
outputs; the Approval Gate Agent that consumes the synthesis before
granting approval.

Excludes: stage artefacts produced within a lifecycle stage (these
are subject to Stage Verifier checks, not multi-perspective review);
bootstrap documents DLMS-2026-0001 through DLMS-2026-0004 which are
exempt pending operational pipeline readiness (bootstrap exception).

## DEFINITIONS

lens:
  A named, scoped analytical perspective assigned to a Perspective
  Reviewer agent. Each lens has a defined subject of focus, a set
  of questions it must answer, and a distinct verdict it produces.
  Lenses are mutually exclusive in focus — two lenses do not
  duplicate each other's checks.

Perspective Reviewer:
  An agent instantiated with a specific lens for a single review
  session. A Perspective Reviewer exists only for the duration of
  the review of one document version. It is context-bounded to its
  lens and the document under review.

review session:
  A single coordinated execution of the multi-perspective review
  stage for one document version. A review session is initiated by
  the Review Orchestrator, runs all mandatory lenses in parallel,
  and concludes when the Review Synthesizer produces its synthesis
  artefact.

lens verdict:
  The outcome produced by a Perspective Reviewer for its assigned
  lens. Values: pass, pass-with-observations, fail. A fail verdict
  blocks advancement to the approval gate. A pass-with-observations
  verdict advances but requires the observations to appear in the
  synthesis artefact.

synthesis artefact:
  The document produced by the Review Synthesizer that consolidates
  all lens verdicts, all observations, all conflicts between lenses,
  and a final review stage recommendation. The synthesis artefact
  is the input to the Approval Gate Agent.

conflict:
  A condition where two Perspective Reviewers produce contradictory
  assessments of the same aspect of a document. Conflicts are not
  errors — they are expected outputs of genuinely independent review.
  All conflicts must be surfaced in the synthesis artefact and
  resolved before approval is granted.

## RULES

### Mandatory_Lenses

R01:
  rule: >
    Every document review session must include all five mandatory
    lenses. No document may advance to the approval gate without
    a complete synthesis artefact covering all five lenses.
  mandatory_lenses:
    lens-accuracy:
      focus: >
        Factual correctness, internal consistency, and compliance with
        higher-tier policies. Checks that all rules, fields, and
        references in the document are correct, non-contradictory,
        and consistent with the policies the document is governed by.
      key_questions:
        - Do all rule tests describe verifiable binary conditions?
        - Are all cross-references to other documents by correct doc_id?
        - Does any rule contradict a higher-tier policy?
        - Are all enum values consistent with their defining schema?
    lens-completeness:
      focus: >
        Whether the document fully addresses its stated scope.
        Checks that no required section is missing, no required field
        is null without justification, and no gap exists between
        the document's stated purpose and its actual content.
      key_questions:
        - Does SCOPE make claims the body does not fulfil?
        - Are all required sections present and substantively populated?
        - Are there edge cases in scope that no rule or requirement
          addresses?
        - Does CHANGE_LOG have an entry for every version?
    lens-clarity:
      focus: >
        Whether the document can be correctly and unambiguously
        consumed by an agent. Checks that terminology is defined,
        rules are unambiguous, and structure follows the template.
      key_questions:
        - Is every term used in RULES defined in DEFINITIONS?
        - Can each rule be parsed to a binary pass/fail test?
        - Does any sentence have more than one plausible reading?
        - Do section headings match the template's required keys exactly?
    lens-economy:
      focus: >
        Whether the document is as concise as it can be while remaining
        complete. Checks for redundancy, duplication between sections,
        and content that could be removed without reducing correctness
        or completeness.
      key_questions:
        - Is any rule restated in a different section without adding
          new information?
        - Does any DEFINITIONS entry define a term not used in the body?
        - Are any CHANGE_LOG entries duplicated or padded?
        - Can any rule be expressed more concisely without loss?
    lens-operability:
      focus: >
        Whether the document is implementable by the agents it governs.
        Checks that every checked_by agent exists in the agent registry,
        every referenced doc_id exists in the DLMS registry, and every
        rule specifies an enforcement mechanism.
      key_questions:
        - Does every rule specify a checked_by agent that exists?
        - Does every document cross-reference resolve to an existing
          approved document?
        - Does every ENFORCEMENT gate_position reference a real
          pipeline stage?
        - Are any acceptance criteria or tests unverifiable in practice?
  test: >
    synthesis artefact contains a verdict entry for all five lenses:
    lens-accuracy, lens-completeness, lens-clarity, lens-economy,
    lens-operability.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Approval Gate Agent

### Reviewer_Independence

R02:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer must be a separate agent invocation
    with a context boundary restricted to its assigned lens and the
    document under review. Reviewers must not share context with
    each other during a review session.
  test: >
    For all review sessions: each Perspective Reviewer has a
    distinct agent_id. No reviewer's context package contains
    another reviewer's lens verdict or intermediate output.
  checked_by: Review Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R03:
  rule: >
    No Perspective Reviewer may be the same agent as the one that
    authored the document under review. This rule applies both to
    the agent identity and the agent role class.
  test: >
    For all review sessions for document D:
    for all Perspective Reviewers PR:
    PR.agent_id != D.created_by AND
    PR.role_class != role_class(D.created_by)
  checked_by: Review Orchestrator (assignment gate);
              Verification Independence Policy R05–R06

### Parallel_Execution

R04:
  rule: >
    All mandatory lens reviews must be executed in parallel within
    a single review session. Sequential execution of lenses is
    prohibited — it allows earlier verdicts to influence later ones,
    violating independence.
  test: >
    For all review sessions: timestamp of first lens start and
    last lens start are within the same session window AND
    no lens verdict is issued before all lenses have started.
  checked_by: Review Orchestrator; Audit Trail Agent

R05:
  rule: >
    Each Perspective Reviewer produces its lens verdict independently,
    without visibility of any other reviewer's verdict or observations
    during the review session. Verdict aggregation occurs only in the
    Review Synthesizer, after all verdicts are complete.
  test: >
    For all review sessions: no lens verdict appears in another
    reviewer's context package during the session. The Review
    Synthesizer receives all verdicts simultaneously after all
    reviewers complete.
  checked_by: Review Orchestrator; Context Delivery Layer;
              Audit Trail Agent

### Verdicts_and_Blocking

R06:
  rule: >
    A single lens-accuracy fail verdict blocks advancement to the
    approval gate. Accuracy failures are non-negotiable — a document
    with factual errors, internal contradictions, or higher-tier
    policy violations may not proceed regardless of other lens verdicts.
  test: >
    If lens-accuracy verdict == fail:
    synthesis artefact recommendation == block AND
    Approval Gate Agent rejects advancement.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Approval Gate Agent

R07:
  rule: >
    A fail verdict from any other mandatory lens (completeness,
    clarity, economy, operability) also blocks advancement to the
    approval gate. All five lenses must return pass or
    pass-with-observations for the document to advance.
  test: >
    For all review sessions:
    if count(verdicts where verdict == fail) > 0:
    synthesis recommendation == block.
    Advancement requires count(verdicts where verdict == fail) == 0.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Approval Gate Agent

R08:
  rule: >
    pass-with-observations verdicts do not block advancement but
    all observations must be recorded in the synthesis artefact
    and in the document's audit trail. The Author Agent receives
    observations and may produce a minor revision to address them
    before the document is approved. Observations are not mandatory
    to resolve before approval — they are advisory.
  test: >
    For all pass-with-observations verdicts:
    observations appear in synthesis artefact AND
    observations are logged as audit entries with
    event_type: review-observation.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Audit Trail Agent

### Conflict_Resolution

R09:
  rule: >
    When two Perspective Reviewers produce contradictory assessments
    of the same aspect of a document, the Review Synthesizer must
    surface the conflict explicitly in the synthesis artefact.
    Conflicts must not be silently resolved by averaging or omission.
  test: >
    For all review sessions where two verdicts address the same
    document element with contradictory assessments:
    synthesis artefact contains a conflicts[] entry naming both
    lenses, the element in dispute, and the nature of the
    contradiction.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Perspective Reviewer (lens-accuracy)
              on the synthesis artefact itself

R10:
  rule: >
    Conflicts between lens verdicts must be resolved before approval
    is granted. Resolution is performed by the Review Synthesizer
    with a documented rationale. If the conflict cannot be resolved
    by the synthesizer, it escalates to SysAdmin for a ruling.
  test: >
    For all conflicts in the synthesis artefact:
    each conflict has a resolution field that is non-null AND
    if resolution == escalated:
    a SysAdmin ruling event exists in the audit trail.
  checked_by: Approval Gate Agent; Audit Trail Agent

### Synthesis_Artefact

R11:
  rule: >
    The Review Synthesizer must produce a synthesis artefact for
    every review session. The synthesis artefact must contain:
    all five lens verdicts, all observations, all conflicts and
    their resolutions, a final recommendation (advance or block),
    and the rationale for the recommendation.
  test: >
    synthesis artefact contains:
    verdicts[5 entries] AND observations[] AND conflicts[] AND
    recommendation IN [advance, block] AND rationale != null.
  checked_by: Approval Gate Agent; Template Validator

R12:
  rule: >
    The synthesis artefact is the sole input to the Approval Gate
    Agent's advancement decision. The Approval Gate Agent must not
    independently re-review the document — it evaluates only the
    synthesis artefact. This enforces the separation between
    review and approval.
  test: >
    Approval Gate Agent's context package for a given document
    contains the synthesis artefact but not the raw lens verdicts
    or the document body.
  checked_by: Context Delivery Layer; Agent Behaviour Analyst

### Additional_Lenses

R13:
  rule: >
    Doc-type-specific lenses may be added to a review session in
    addition to the five mandatory lenses. Additional lenses are
    declared in the relevant template's VALIDATION_RULES section
    under checked_by: Perspective Reviewer ([lens-name]).
    Additional lenses follow all rules that apply to mandatory lenses.
  test: >
    For all additional lenses declared in a template:
    the corresponding Perspective Reviewer is included in the
    review session for documents of that doc_type.
  checked_by: Review Orchestrator (session composition check)

R14:
  rule: >
    Additional lenses may not substitute for mandatory lenses.
    The five mandatory lenses are required for every document
    regardless of doc_type. Additional lenses are additive only.
  test: >
    count(mandatory lens verdicts in synthesis artefact) == 5
    regardless of how many additional lenses were run.
  checked_by: Review Synthesizer; Approval Gate Agent

## EXCEPTIONS_PROCESS
No exceptions process applies to this policy for the five mandatory
lenses. Multi-perspective review is a structural invariant of the
DLMS approval process — a document that has not passed all five lenses
may not be approved under any circumstances. The bootstrap exception
declared in SCOPE is the only recognised deviation.

Additional lenses (R13) may be waived on a per-document basis via a
documented SysAdmin directive where operational urgency requires it.
Waiver of an additional lens must be recorded in the synthesis artefact
and the audit trail. This waiver path does not extend to mandatory lenses.

## ENFORCEMENT
  primary_agent:    Review Orchestrator; Review Synthesizer
  gate_positions:
    - Review stage entry: Review Orchestrator validates reviewer
      independence (R02–R03) before any lens invocation
    - Parallel launch: Review Orchestrator enforces R04 at session start
    - Synthesis gate: Review Synthesizer enforces R06–R08, R09–R11
      before producing the synthesis artefact
    - Approval gate: Approval Gate Agent enforces R12 — consumes
      only synthesis artefact, not raw verdicts
  audit: >
    All review events are logged by Audit Trail Agent:
    review-session-started, lens-assigned, lens-verdict-issued,
    review-observation, review-conflict-detected,
    review-conflict-resolved, review-synthesis-completed,
    review-block-issued, review-advance-issued.
    Each lens verdict is an individual audit entry referencing
    the session's audit_ref.
  violation_type:   Tier 2 principles violation
  escalation: >
    Unresolvable lens conflicts escalate to sysadmin:dlm-sysadmin.
    Bypass of the review stage (document advancing without a synthesis
    artefact) is a critical integrity violation escalated immediately
    via SysAdmin Escalation Policy.

## REVIEW_SCHEDULE
  interval_max:      12 months (Tier 2 maximum)
  trigger_conditions:
    - A new doc_type is added whose review requirements are not
      covered by the five mandatory lenses
    - A CI cycle identifies a systematic pattern of pass-with-observations
      across one lens suggesting the lens criteria need sharpening
    - A conflict resolution is escalated to SysAdmin more than twice
      in a single CI period, suggesting a lens boundary ambiguity
    - A new agent capability makes parallel execution constraints
      technically moot (requires policy re-evaluation)
    - Agent Behaviour Analyst detects that lens verdicts are
      becoming systematically correlated across review sessions
      (suggesting loss of true independence)
